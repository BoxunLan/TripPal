CIIE service guide: Dining solutions for international visitors

english.shanghai.gov.cn |
October 16, 2025

Editor's note: Traveling to a new country can raise many questions. How do I pay? How do I get around? To ensure every international guest feels welcome and supported, the CIIE service initiative offers practical solutions for transportation, payment, dining, tax-free shopping, and hotel stays. Unlock a seamless CIIE experience with us!

In this episode, join Scott at a restaurant in the National Exhibition and Convention Center as he walks you through how to order a meal with ease. Can't read the Chinese menu? No problem. Here are two simple ways to order.

​

Option 1: Use the "Easy Go" platform on Alipay

Simply open the "Easy Go" service page in your Alipay app, scan the menu using the built-in translation tool, and get an instant English translation. It's fast and accurate.

​

​

"Easy Go" offers more than just translation. It's your all-in-one companion in Shanghai for public transport, ride-hailing, food delivery, attraction tickets, shopping, and instant tax refunds.

Option 2: Ask the staff for help

Many restaurant staff at the CIIE venue speak English and are ready to assist you in ordering. Don't hesitate to ask — they're here to help!

​

Whether you order with your phone or chat with the staff, a tasty meal is just around the corner. Enjoy every bite and your time dining in Shanghai!
