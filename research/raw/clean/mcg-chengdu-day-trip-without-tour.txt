Every tour agency in Chengdu will try to sell you a ¥450-680 day trip to Dujiangyan, Mount Qingcheng, or the Panda Base with lunch and a guide in broken English. I have lived in Chengdu long enough to have met people who did both the tour and the independent version, and the independent version is cheaper, less rushed, and lets you actually spend time at the places that interest you. This is the Chengdu day trip I recommend to every friend visiting, because it is the easiest independent day trip in Sichuan and the best single day of sightseeing you can do without leaving the Chengdu metro area.
My go-to day trip from Chengdu without a tour is Dujiangyan Irrigation System in the morning, Mount Qingcheng (front mountain) in the afternoon, both connected by the same high-speed rail line that leaves from Xipu Station (犀浦), at the western end of Metro Line 2. Total cost is ¥220-300 per person depending on cable cars and lunch. This guide covers that core route plus the other four day trips from Chengdu I have done independently, with real 2026 prices.

The main event: Dujiangyan + Mount Qingcheng combined day trip
Dujiangyan Irrigation System is a 2,300-year-old waterworks project that still functions today. It is a UNESCO World Heritage site and has engineering that is genuinely impressive once you understand what you are looking at. Mount Qingcheng is the birthplace of Daoism, dotted with temples, and the front mountain is hikeable in half a day. Both are on the same high-speed rail line from Chengdu, which is why this is the best day trip from Chengdu without a tour you can do.
The route. Xipu Station (犀浦), at the western end of Metro Line 2 → HSR to Dujiangyan Station → visit Dujiangyan Irrigation System → HSR from Dujiangyan to Qingchengshan Station → visit Mount Qingcheng front mountain → HSR back to Chengdu.

Timing. Leave your Chengdu hotel at 7 AM. Be at Xipu Station by 7:45. Board an HSR around 8:00. Arrive Dujiangyan by 8:45. You want to be at the Dujiangyan park gate by 9:15 to beat the tour groups. Spend 2.5 hours at Dujiangyan including lunch at the Fish Mouth area. Catch the 12:30 HSR from Dujiangyan to Qingchengshan. Arrive at Qingchengshan park gate by 1:15. Spend 4-5 hours on Mount Qingcheng front mountain. Take one of the several trains back to Xipu in the early evening; check 12306 for the day. Home by 8 PM.
Dujiangyan Irrigation System: what to actually see
The park is bigger than most visitors expect. The core sights are the Fish Mouth (Yuzui) dyke, the Flying Sand Weir, the Bottle Neck channel, and the Erwang Temple honoring Li Bing, the engineer who designed the system in 256 BC. You can walk the whole route in 2 hours if you move efficiently.
My standard Dujiangyan route:
Enter at the main gate near Lidui Park.

Walk through Fulong Temple (skip unless you love temples).

Cross the Anlan suspension bridge over the river. It sways. This is fun.

Walk along the Yuzui embankment to see the Fish Mouth separation dyke.

Continue to Erwang Temple for the view and the Li Bing statue.

Exit via Erwang Temple's back gate if you want the shortcut to town.

Lunch inside the park is overpriced and mediocre. Skip it. Eat a quick noodle bowl in the town strip 10 minutes from the park entrance for ¥15-25. There are also good beef noodle shops near the bus stop. The Dujiangyan area is known for its twice-cooked pork (huiguorou), which is ¥28-40 at a sit-down place.
Mount Qingcheng front mountain: cable car or hike?
Mount Qingcheng has two halves. The front mountain (Qianshan) is the famous Daoist temples, heavily trafficked, cable car access, can be done in 4-5 hours. The back mountain (Houshan) is a much quieter hike, no famous temples, requires 6-8 hours to appreciate, and is worth visiting only if you have a full day specifically for Qingcheng.
For a day trip from Chengdu without a tour that combines with Dujiangyan, you want the front mountain. Here is the cable car decision.
Option A: Take the cable car up, walk down (recommended for day trip). Cable car one way is ¥35, round trip is ¥60. You still pay the ¥5 electric boat across Yuecheng Lake to reach the cable car base. From the top station it is 20 minutes of flat walking to Shangqing Temple, which is the main sight. Walking down via the ancient stone steps takes about 90 minutes and passes smaller temples, moss-covered bridges, and tea houses where you can stop for ¥15 local tea.
Option B: Walk up and down. This is 5-6 hours total and you will be tired. Skip the day trip part of this plan if you choose this.
Option C: Cable car both ways. Fastest. Saves you 90 minutes but you miss the stone path which is the whole vibe. Only do this if your knees are unhappy or the weather is bad.
I always do Option A. The walk down is the best part of the mountain.
Chengdu Panda Base: not a great full day trip, but worth half a day
The Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding is inside the Chengdu metropolitan area (not really a day trip in the traditional sense, more a half-day excursion) and pandas are most active in the morning. Go early. Here is what a Panda Base morning looks like.
Arrive at 7:30 AM (park opens at 7:30). Ticket is ¥55 in 2026. The pandas are fed at 8:30-9:30 AM and this is when they are outside and moving. By 11 AM most pandas have returned to their air-conditioned indoor dens and you will stare at a lump of fur sleeping.
Getting there from downtown: Metro Line 3 to Panda Avenue station, or Didi for ¥35-50 from People's Square. A shuttle from the metro station is ¥2. Total Panda Base cost including transport and entry is ¥70-100 per person.
You can do Panda Base in the morning and Dujiangyan in the afternoon technically, but the logistics are tight because Panda Base is northeast and Dujiangyan is northwest. I prefer either Panda Base alone as a half day (pair with Wuhou Temple or Jinli Street in the afternoon) or the Dujiangyan-Qingcheng combo alone as a full day.
Leshan Giant Buddha day trip: worth it but a full day
The Leshan Giant Buddha is the world's largest carved stone Buddha, 71 meters tall, chiseled into a cliff at the confluence of three rivers. It is stunning in person. A Leshan day trip from Chengdu without a tour is feasible but uses the entire day.
Leshan Buddha route: Chengdu East → Leshan HSR (¥54 second class, 1 hour). Leshan Station → Dafosi (Giant Buddha scenic area) via bus K1 or Didi (¥10-25, 20 minutes). Entry is ¥80 in 2026. You must decide whether to walk down the cliff stairs for the close-up view (2-hour wait in peak season due to one-way single-file stairs) or take the boat for a river-level view (¥70, 30 minutes, much less crowded). I take the boat every time. The boat view is better for photos and faster.
Total Leshan day trip cost: about ¥250-290 per person. Total time: 9-10 hours door to door. You cannot combine Leshan with Mount Emei in a single day from Chengdu without sacrificing both. Pick one.
Bifengxia Panda Base day trip: quieter, with more pandas outdoors
If you have already done the Chengdu Panda Base and want another panda experience, Bifengxia in Ya'an is the less crowded sister base. It has more pandas, bigger enclosures, and the pandas are outdoors longer because the climate is cooler. The trade-off is distance: Chengdu to Bifengxia is 2.5 hours each way by bus or a mix of HSR + taxi. This is a legitimate full-day trip.
Bifengxia day trip from Chengdu without a tour cost: about ¥280-340 per person including entry (¥110 in 2026). It is a long day but rewarding if you are a serious panda fan. The Ya'an area also has some decent mountain scenery.

What to do if the weather is bad
Chengdu gets fog, rain, and overcast weeks, especially in winter and early spring. Here are weather-adaptive day trip from Chengdu alternatives:
Rainy day : skip mountain hikes. Do Sanxingdui Museum (indoor), Chengdu Museum (indoor), Kuanzhai Alley + Jinli Street (partially covered).

Heavy fog : skip Mount Qingcheng (views are gone). Do Dujiangyan alone (it is more about engineering than views) + Panda Base or Wuhou Temple.

Freezing : skip long outdoor stairs. Do the hotpot crawl instead, or Anshun ancient town.

Burning hot (July-August) : do Mount Qingcheng for the shade and tea houses. Avoid Dujiangyan midday.

What is the best single day trip from Chengdu without a tour? Dujiangyan Irrigation System plus Mount Qingcheng front mountain, combined on the same HSR line. Total cost ¥220-300 per person. Duration 10-12 hours door to door.
Can I do Leshan and Mount Emei in one day from Chengdu? Technically possible on the same HSR line (Chengdu East → Emeishan stops at Leshan). Realistically it means rushing both. I recommend picking one, or staying overnight in Emeishan town.
Is the Chengdu Panda Base worth a half day or full day? Half day max. Pandas are active 7:30-10:30 AM. After 11 AM they sleep and you see nothing. Combine with in-city sights in the afternoon.
Do I need to book HSR tickets in advance? For the Chengdu-Dujiangyan intercity line, no. Trains run every 20-30 minutes and tickets are almost always available at the station. For Chengdu-Leshan or Chengdu-Emeishan, book at least a day ahead on Trip.com or 12306, especially on weekends.
How do I buy park tickets as a foreigner? Both Dujiangyan and Mount Qingcheng sell tickets at the main entrance counter with passport ID. Alipay, WeChat Pay , and cash all work. Some counters accept foreign Visa/Mastercard. The WeChat mini-programs for both parks also sell tickets if you have WeChat set up.
Can I speak only English on this day trip from Chengdu without a tour? Yes. HSR stations have English signage and ticket machines. Park maps have English. Pleco handles any menu. Didi has an English setting. You will be fine.
What should I wear for Mount Qingcheng? Grippy shoes, layered clothing. The stone stairs get slippery when wet. Temperature is 8-12°C cooler than Chengdu on average. A light rain jacket is smart.
