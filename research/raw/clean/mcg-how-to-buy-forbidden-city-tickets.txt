Visiting the Forbidden City: Your Complete Ticket Guide
Walking through the Meridian Gate into the Forbidden City is a highlight of any trip to Beijing. However, securing entry to the Palace Museum has become increasingly competitive in recent years. With no tickets sold at the gate and strict identity checks, planning ahead is not just recommended—it is mandatory. This guide answers the most common questions about where to book, when to buy, and how to ensure your visit goes smoothly.

Official vs. Third-Party Platforms: Where to Buy
One of the most frequent concerns for travelers is identifying the correct website amidst many lookalikes. There are two primary paths to securing a ticket: the direct official channel and authorized third-party agents.
The Official Channel
The only primary source for face-value tickets is the Palace Museum Official Website .
Chinese Interface: https://ticket.dpm.org.cn/

English Portal: https://en.dpm.org.cn/ (Navigate to "Visit" then "Buy Tickets")

WeChat Mini Program: Search for "故宫博物院" within the WeChat app.

Pros: You pay the standard entry price without markups. Cons: The system can be glitchy for foreign passports, and payment often requires Alipay or WeChat Pay . The English portal sometimes experiences technical delays with verification codes.
The Reliable Third-Party Option
If you encounter issues with the official site or lack Chinese payment methods, Trip.com is widely considered the most reliable authorized agent for international travelers.
Pros: They accept international credit cards (Visa/Mastercard), offer English customer support, and sometimes have access to tour-group quotas when individual tickets are sold out. Cons: You will pay a service fee on top of the ticket price.
Verdict: Try the official website first to save money. If the system rejects your passport details or payment fails, switch to a trusted agent like Trip.com immediately rather than risking scalpers.
Timing Your Booking: The 7-Day Rule
Understanding the release schedule is critical. Tickets are not sold months in advance; they are released on a rolling basis.
Booking Window: Tickets open 7 days prior to your visit.

Release Time: 20:00 (8:00 PM) Beijing Time .

Sell-Out Speed: During peak travel seasons, tickets often vanish within 5 to 10 minutes of release.

Pro Tip: Calculate the exact time difference between your location and Beijing. Log in to your chosen platform at least 5 minutes before the release window opens. Have your passport details copied and ready to paste to save seconds.

Essential Entry Rules and Regulations
To avoid being turned away at the gate, you must adhere to strict museum policies.
1. Identification Requirements
The Forbidden City operates on a strict real-name system. You must bring the original physical passport used during booking. Digital copies or photos of your passport are not accepted for entry. Ensure the name on the ticket matches your passport exactly, character for character.
2. Entrance and Exit Points
Many first-time visitors make the mistake of going to the wrong gate.
Entry: You must enter through the Meridian Gate (Wu Men) , which is the South Gate.

Exit: You must exit through the Gate of Divine Prowess (Shenwu Men) at the North, or the East/West Flowery Gates. You cannot leave through the entrance.

3. Closed Days
The museum is closed every Monday , unless that Monday coincides with a Chinese public holiday. Plan your itinerary accordingly to avoid arriving on a closed day.
4. Time Slots
Tickets are assigned to specific entry slots: Morning or Afternoon .
Morning: Enter before 12:00 PM.

Afternoon: Enter after 12:00 PM.

If you hold a morning ticket, you will be denied entry in the afternoon.
Practical Tips for a Smooth Visit
Avoid Scalpers: Do not purchase tickets from individuals on social media or touts near the museum. These are often scams, and mismatched names will result in denied entry.

Cancellation Policy: Official tickets can usually be cancelled up to 8:00 PM on the day before your visit for a full refund. Third-party policies vary, so check the terms before booking.

Payment Preparation: If using the official site, ensure your Alipay or WeChat Pay is set up and funded beforehand. International cards on the English portal may fail during high traffic.

Connectivity: Have your ticket confirmation QR code screenshot and saved to your phone gallery. Internet connectivity can be spotty near the entrance.

Final Thoughts
Securing a ticket to the Forbidden City requires preparation, but the experience of walking through centuries of imperial history is worth the effort. By booking exactly seven days in advance at 8:00 PM Beijing Time and ensuring your passport details are accurate, you can bypass the stress and focus on the adventure. Whether you choose the official portal or a trusted agent, early action is the key to unlocking the gates of the Palace Museum.
