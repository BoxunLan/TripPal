China Eastern Economy Seat Selection: Free at Booking, Front Rows Cost Extra

On a China Eastern economy ticket you can pick a free seat as soon as the ticket is issued, on China Eastern's website or app, for domestic and some international flights operated by China Eastern or Shanghai Airlines. The catch is that a large part of the map is held back: the front rows, the exit rows and many window and aisle seats cost points or cash. Online check-in, which issues the boarding pass, opens 48 hours before departure on most routes and 24 hours before on US and Canada routes.
Everything below is about which seats are held back, what they cost, and when they return to the free map.

Reserve as soon as your ticket is issued, since other passengers can take the free seats from that point. If advance selection is not offered for your flight, set an alarm for the minute check-in opens. The prices above are China Eastern's list prices; it runs seat promotions, so the price on the payment screen is the one that counts.
There is a second release worth knowing about. Once check-in opens, China Eastern frees part of the held-back stock, and 6 hours before departure it releases the unsold seats to the free map, except row 1 and the exit rows. If the map looks empty, check again at those two points. Some seats stay closed even after release for safety or operational reasons; you can ask for those at the airport counter.

What you cannot buy on a domestic flight
On domestic routes China Eastern sells preferred seats for miles, not cash. If you are a foreign traveller without a MU account holding miles, the front rows and extra-legroom seats are simply not purchasable — no amount of trying different cards will change it. Take one of the free seats instead.
Exit rows have their own gate. You must be at least 15, physically able to assist, and able to follow crew instructions. Travelling with an infant disqualifies you outright, regardless of age.
Why every seat looks locked
In late 2024 China Eastern drew a wave of coverage in the Chinese press after passengers found that at check-in almost the entire map appeared unavailable — the 锁座 problem. Some of that is the preferred-seat block described above; some of it is inventory management.
Two consequences worth planning around. A seat you selected is not guaranteed: China Eastern's rules say a flight or aircraft change, or a safety or operational reason, can change your seat number, and for held-back seats the airline will try to find an equivalent one and tell you by text message. And a paid seat you lose is not refunded on its own: apply through the channel you bought it from, and expect no refund if staff give you a seat of equal value or an upgrade. Re-check your seat once boarding is announced.
Held-back seats are still a large share of the map. In a November 2025 survey, the Jiangsu Consumer Council bought tickets and chose seats in ten airlines' official apps; on the China Eastern route it checked, between 40% and 46% of economy seats were locked at the booking stage.
On 29 January 2026 the China Air Transport Association (CATA), the national industry association for air transport, published a draft standard on reserved seats and took comments until 27 February. As drafted, at least 70% of economy seats on domestic flights and 65% on international and regional flights would have to be free to choose. Domestic flights could hold seats back for points or miles but could not sell them for cash, so cash seat fees would stay limited to international and regional routes. Airlines would also have to show which seats are free, what the others cost and when held seats are released. It is an industry standard rather than a law, and CATA had not announced a final version as of September 2026, so China Eastern's current rules above still apply.
Selecting the seat, and what breaks
On ceair.com, open Seat Selection & Check-in and enter your 13-digit ticket number or passport number, plus your name exactly as ticketed; the app and the WeChat and Alipay mini-programs have the same function. On the website's seat map, seats marked with a $ sign are the held-back ones that cost points or cash, and the other open seats are free. Pick, confirm, and once check-in opens, download the boarding pass.
Payment is a separate problem for foreign visitors. China Eastern's seat-selection guide says its online seat payment does not currently accept foreign bank cards, so a card issued outside China may not work for a Preferred Seat on an international flight. Check which payment methods the page offers before you count on buying one online; China Eastern also sells these seats at its check-in counter at the departure airport on the day you fly.

"Seat selection not available"

Advance selection covers domestic and only some international routes, and only flights operated by China Eastern or Shanghai Airlines. Wait for check-in

Ticket issued by a partner airline

ceair.com may not find a ticket issued by another airline. Contact the airline that issued it

Group split across rows

Add your companions on the same flight (up to five) when you select seats, so you can pick seats together. If that fails, call China Eastern's 24-hour global hotline, +86-21-20695530 (95530 from a Chinese number), which has an English option

After check-in you can cancel or change your seat online up to three times. After that, further changes go through the airport counter or China Eastern customer service. A paid seat cannot be switched directly: cancel it, which is free, and buy the new one.
If you miss the window
The airport counter still assigns seats free of charge, subject to what is left. Arrive two hours early for a domestic flight, three for international — which is also roughly what a Chengdu connection needs if this is a transfer rather than an origin.

For the domestic network more broadly, flying domestically in China as a foreigner covers ID checks, the 45-minute domestic cut-off and what happens when a flight is delayed — which on Chinese domestic routes is often.
Baggage, because it changes where you want to sit
Cabin: one bag up to 8 kg and 55 × 40 × 20 cm, plus a personal item that fits under the seat in front. Overhead space on the A320 and B737 domestic fleet runs out fast, which is an argument for a lower row number rather than a better view.

Checked: 20 kg in total on domestic flights; on international flights each piece can weigh up to 23 kg, and the number of free pieces (none to two) depends on the route and fare brand.

Paid seat fees do not always come back on their own. If your ticket is refunded or changed, the seat is cancelled; if the fee is not refunded automatically, apply through the channel you bought it from, within 12 months of buying it.

FAQ
Can I choose my China Eastern economy seat when I book?
Yes, on China Eastern's website or app, for domestic and some international flights operated by China Eastern or Shanghai Airlines, but only from the seats left free. Front rows, exit rows and many window and aisle seats are held back for points on domestic flights and for cash or points on international ones. Online check-in opens 48 hours before departure, or 24 hours on US and Canada routes. China Eastern's rules give the held-back seats free to Eastern Miles Platinum members, not to SkyTeam Elite or Elite Plus members as such.
How much is an exit row seat on China Eastern?
On domestic flights it is not sold for cash: row 1 and exit-row seats cost 2,000 Eastern Miles points. On international flights the list price is ¥300 on short and medium-haul routes, ¥900 long-haul and ¥200 on Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan routes. You must be at least 15 and physically able to assist, and travelling with an infant disqualifies you.
Why can't I pay for a better seat on a domestic MU flight?
Because on domestic routes China Eastern sells preferred seats for miles rather than cash. Without a MU account holding miles there is no way to buy one; take one of the free seats instead.
My card keeps being declined for the seat fee. What now?
China Eastern's seat-selection guide says its online seat payment does not accept foreign cards. Check which other payment methods the page offers, or buy the seat at the China Eastern check-in counter on the day of travel.
Can I change my seat after selecting it?
Up to three times online after check-in; after that, ask at the airport counter or call China Eastern customer service. A paid seat cannot be switched directly: cancel it, which is free, and buy the new one.
