Hello, do foreigners also have Beijing Park Sightseeing Passes? If yes, where can foreigners buy that?

2025-07-22

Hello. The annual passes for Beijing's parks are now available in two types, namely the Beijing Park Sightseeing Passes (北京市公园游览年票; CNY 100 each) and Combined Annual Passes for Parks in Beijing and Cultural and Leisure Attractions in Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei Region (北京市公园游览年票、京津冀名胜文化休闲旅游年卡联合年卡; CNY 200 each).

According to the "2025 Notice on Sales of Annual Passes for Beijing's Parks", labor union members and retirees can buy the Beijing Park Sightseeing Passes (CNY 100 each); others can buy the Combined Annual Passes for Parks in Beijing and Cultural and Leisure Attractions in Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei Region (CNY 200 each).

An applicant can bring his/her Beijing Card - Mutual Aid Service Card (京卡·互助服务卡) or any other certificate proving that he/she is a labor union member, and his/her recent bareheaded photo to apply for a physical annual pass at the ticketing office of Zhongshan Park, Jingshan Park, Taoranting Park, or Yuyuantan Park.

If you don't have a Beijing Card - Mutual Aid Service Card or any other certificate proving that you are a labor union member, you can bring a recent photo of yourself and apply for a CNY-200 physical annual pass at one of the aforementioned ticketing offices.

Thank you for your understanding and support!

Beijing Municipal Administration Center of Parks
