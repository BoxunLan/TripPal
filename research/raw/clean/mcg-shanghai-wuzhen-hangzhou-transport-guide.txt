The easiest Shanghai → Wuzhen → Hangzhou route in 2026 is: high-speed rail (HSR) from Shanghai Hongqiao to Tongxiang (¥58-92, 30-45 min) + bus K282 or taxi to Wuzhen (¥4-60, 30 min), overnight, then HSR Tongxiang → Hangzhou East (¥30-45, 25 min). Total transit cost is under ¥200 per person and the whole loop fits in 2.5-4 days.

Option 1: Shanghai to Wuzhen by high-speed rail
The fastest and most flexible route is via Tongxiang (桐乡) HSR station on the Shanghai-Kunming line.
Shanghai Hongqiao → Tongxiang
Stations : Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station (上海虹桥站), accessible by Metro Line 2/10/17 from central Shanghai.

Trains : 30+ G and D class high-speed trains daily.

Duration : 30-45 minutes.

Fare : ¥58-69 second class, ¥92-110 first class, ¥185 business class.

Booking : 12306 English site/app or Trip.com. Passport as ID. Show passport + booking at the automated gate.

Arrive at Hongqiao 45 minutes before departure — the station is massive and security lines can stretch 10-20 minutes.
Tongxiang station → Wuzhen
Tongxiang HSR station is 13 km from Wuzhen East Scenic Zone and 16 km from Wuzhen West Scenic Zone. Three transfer options:
Bus K282 — ¥4, 25-40 min, runs every 15-30 min from 6 am to 6 pm from the bus plaza outside Tongxiang station. This is the line most locals use.

Official Wuzhen shuttle — ¥20, 25 min, runs every 30 min, leaves from Tongxiang station north exit. Stops directly at both East and West Scenic Zone ticket gates.

Taxi / Didi — ¥40-60 to East, ¥50-70 to West, 20-25 minutes. Drivers at the taxi rank accept QR pay and most take foreign passports.

Direct bus from Shanghai
If you have lots of luggage or miss the HSR window, the Shanghai South Long-Distance Bus Station (上海长途汽车客运南站) runs 4-6 direct coaches daily to Wuzhen East Scenic Zone. Price ¥100, duration 2-2.5 hours, booked through the station counter or the Keyun 12308 app. The HSR + shuttle combo is usually both faster and cheaper.
Option 2: Wuzhen to Hangzhou
Wuzhen sits roughly halfway between Shanghai and Hangzhou, so the second leg is shorter than the first.
HSR via Tongxiang
Most visitors return to Tongxiang station and take HSR onward to Hangzhou East (杭州东站):
Duration : 23-35 minutes.

Fare : ¥30-43 second class, ¥48-69 first class.

Trains : 20+ daily, all day.

Add the 25-40 min transfer from Wuzhen back to Tongxiang station, so plan 1.5-2 hours door-to-door including buffer.
Direct bus to Hangzhou Jiubao Bus Station
A local coach runs from Wuzhen West Scenic Zone bus plaza directly to Hangzhou Jiubao Passenger Transport Center (杭州九堡客运中心) several times a day:
Fare : ¥40.

Duration : 90 minutes.

Tickets : Counter at Wuzhen West bus plaza or the 12308 app.

Jiubao is 15 km east of West Lake and is on Hangzhou Metro Line 1 (¥4-6 to central Hangzhou).
Private car / Didi
A Didi Premier booked from Wuzhen East to central Hangzhou costs ¥200-260 and takes 60-75 minutes. Useful for families with 3+ bags, because swapping buses with luggage in Tongxiang is painful.

Wuzhen East vs West Scenic Zone tickets
Wuzhen is split into two paid enclosures. You need tickets to enter either.
Wuzhen East Scenic Zone (乌镇东栅) : ¥110 day ticket. Smaller, daytime-only, easy half-day walk of canals, a rice-wine distillery, and an indigo dye workshop. Closes around 5:30 pm.

Wuzhen West Scenic Zone (乌镇西栅) : ¥150 day ticket or ¥190 combo (East + West). Larger, open until 10 pm with atmospheric night lighting on canals. This is the one most international travelers spend their night in.

Hotels inside West Scenic Zone : Staying inside the enclosure (¥400-1500/night) includes repeat entry for the length of your stay. Reserve via Trip.com, Booking.com, or the official Wuzhen site.

Buy tickets at the gate counter (passports accepted) or on Trip.com/Wuzhen official Alipay mini-program. Save a QR code or screenshot.
Luggage storage
Don't drag wheeled bags over Wuzhen's stone canal bridges.
Shanghai Hongqiao Station — paid storage lockers in the basement, ¥30-50 per bag per day.

Tongxiang HSR Station — small counter left of the main exit, ¥15-25 per bag, cash or Alipay.

Wuzhen East Scenic Zone gate — free same-day lockers if space is available.

Wuzhen West Scenic Zone — guest room drop-off if you have booked inside; outside gate counter ¥20-30 if you are only visiting.

Hangzhou East Station — automated lockers, ¥40-80 per 24 hours.

Shanghai to Wuzhen pitfalls to avoid
Booking HSR to the wrong station : Tongxiang serves Wuzhen; Jiaxing South does not. Double-check the station name on 12306 before paying.

Taking the wrong bus from Tongxiang : The K282 public bus and the official shuttle both work, but the unofficial "tourist vans" outside the station charge ¥80-100 and often drop you far from the scenic zone entrance. Use the official ¥20 shuttle counter at the north exit.

Missing the East vs West distinction : They share a name, not a location. East Scenic Zone is smaller and closes earlier; West Scenic Zone is the night-time atmospheric one. Most people want West.

Forgetting bag storage rules : Wuzhen's stone canal bridges are brutal for rolling bags. Stash the big suitcase in Shanghai or Hongqiao station.

Skipping passport on HSR : The passport used to book is the only ID that will open the gate. Children need their own passports listed as separate tickets.

3-day Jiangnan loop itinerary
Day 1 — Shanghai : Fly into Shanghai Pudong (PVG). Metro Line 2 to People's Square (¥8) or Maglev + Metro. Afternoon at the Bund and Nanjing Road, evening Yuyuan Garden and Xintiandi.

Day 2 morning — Shanghai : Former French Concession walk, M50 Art District, Jing'an Temple, Shanghai Museum (free, reserve on WeChat mini-program the day before).

Day 2 afternoon — to Wuzhen : 1 pm HSR Shanghai Hongqiao → Tongxiang, shuttle to Wuzhen West Scenic Zone, check into hotel, evening canal walk with lanterns.

Day 3 morning — Wuzhen : Sunrise canal walk, Wuzhen opera stage, Sanbai Wine distillery, indigo dye yard. Check out by 11 am.

Day 3 afternoon — to Hangzhou : Transfer back to Tongxiang, HSR to Hangzhou East, check into hotel near West Lake (Hubin area).

Day 3 evening — Hangzhou : West Lake sunset walk from Su Causeway, dinner at a Hangzhou restaurant near Hefang Street.

Day 4 — Hangzhou (add if you have a 4th day): Early ferry at West Lake, Lingyin Temple (¥30 park + ¥30 temple), Longjing tea village, HSR back to Shanghai Hongqiao in the evening.

Booking apps for foreigners in 2026
12306 — The official Chinese Railways site and app. English version works for HSR tickets with passport. No commission.

Trip.com — English app that sells 12306 tickets with a small ¥5-20 service fee. Accepts foreign Visa/Mastercard.

Ctrip — Chinese version of Trip.com, same rail inventory.

Amap (高德地图) — For in-country directions and public transit routes. English UI buried in settings.

Didi Chuxing — Ride-hailing app with English version for foreign users. Works in Shanghai, Wuzhen, and Hangzhou city limits.

Alipay / WeChat Pay — Both support foreign cards for domestic payments since 2023; top up in the app to pay for tickets, buses, and food stalls.

Where to stay on the Jiangnan loop
Shanghai
The Bund / Waitan — Classic colonial facades, great for first-time visitors, Metro Line 2/10. Mid-range ¥600-1000, high ¥1500+.

Xintiandi / Former French Concession — Quieter, leafy, cafés, near Metro Line 10/13. Mid-range ¥500-900.

Near Shanghai Hongqiao station — Practical for an early HSR out to Wuzhen. ¥350-600.

Wuzhen
Inside West Scenic Zone — Stay after 5 pm when day-trippers leave and the streets empty. ¥500-1200, booked via Trip.com or the official Wuzhen site. Your room key doubles as a West Scenic Zone re-entry ticket.

Outside the gates — Cheaper (¥150-350) but you pay the ¥150 daily entry every time you want to see the canals.

Hangzhou
Hubin / Yanggong Causeway — Walking distance to West Lake. Mid-range ¥500-900, high ¥1200-2500.

Xihu Xijing (West Lake West) — Quieter, near Lingyin Temple. ¥400-700.

Near Hangzhou East station — Practical for onward HSR; ¥300-500 but 25-minute metro ride to West Lake.

Book through Trip.com (English) or Agoda for foreign card support. Booking.com and Hotels.com cover the same inventory at similar rates.

How long does the Shanghai to Hangzhou bullet train take?
Direct G-class high-speed trains between Shanghai Hongqiao and Hangzhou East take 45-60 minutes. Second-class fares run ¥73-87, first class ¥117-140. Over 60 trains run daily from early morning to around 10 pm. No stop in Wuzhen — for Wuzhen you take a separate HSR to Tongxiang first.
Which Wuzhen scenic zone is better, East or West?
West Scenic Zone is larger, stays open until 10 pm for lantern-lit night photography, and has hotels inside the walls. East Scenic Zone is smaller, cheaper (¥110 vs ¥150), and closes late afternoon — better if you are just doing a half-day visit. Most international travelers pick West or buy the ¥190 combo ticket to see both.
Is there a direct high-speed train from Shanghai to Wuzhen?
No. Wuzhen does not have an HSR station. The nearest HSR station is Tongxiang (桐乡), 13-16 km from the scenic zones. Take HSR Shanghai Hongqiao → Tongxiang (¥58-69, 30-45 min), then bus K282 or the official ¥20 shuttle to Wuzhen.
Can I buy Shanghai-Tongxiang HSR tickets with a foreign passport?
Yes. Use 12306 (official), Trip.com, or Ctrip with passport as ID. Tickets are e-tickets — your passport at the automated gate opens the turnstile. Keep the passport used to book matched to the traveler. Book 2-7 days in advance for high season weekends and holidays.
What's the best way to carry luggage between Shanghai, Wuzhen, and Hangzhou?
Use 20-22 inch hand-carry rolling bags and leave checked luggage at a Shanghai hotel storage room or a 12306 luggage counter for the Wuzhen night. Most Shanghai hotels hold bags for returning guests free of charge. Inside Wuzhen scenic zones, wheeled bags struggle on the stone bridges — pack one small overnight bag plus day essentials for the Wuzhen leg.
