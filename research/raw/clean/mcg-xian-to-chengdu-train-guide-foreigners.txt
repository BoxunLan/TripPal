Xi'an to Chengdu by Train: Times, Fares and Stops on the Way

The high-speed train is the way to go between Xi'an and Chengdu. Several trains an hour run from Xi'an North to Chengdu East. The fastest takes 3 hours 5 minutes and most take between 3.5 and 4 hours. On the dates checked for this guide, second class cost ¥263 to ¥316 on almost every train, and first class ¥421 to ¥506.
The Xi'an–Chengdu high-speed railway opened in December 2017 as the first high-speed line through the Qinling Mountains. Before it, the fastest trip between the two cities took more than 10 hours. Much of the ride is underground: tunnels make up 55 percent of the line and 95 percent of the section through the Qinling, according to Xinhua at the opening.
Times, fares and seat availability below come from China Railway's 12306 booking system, searched on 27 September 2026 for travel on Tuesday 29 September and Friday 9 October 2026. Fares differ from train to train.

Xi'an North to Chengdu East on Tuesday 29 September 2026, from a 12306 search on 27 September 2026. China Railway adjusts timetables in October, so check 12306 for the trains running on your date.

The other direction is much the same. On the same day, about as many trains ran from Chengdu East to Xi'an North, from about 6 a.m. to about 8 p.m., the fastest in 3 hours 9 minutes, at the same fares.
Two timing details are worth knowing. On 29 September the earliest departures from Xi'an North, soon after 5 a.m., were overnight sleeper trains from Beijing and Shanghai passing through, and their seats were already sold out. At the other end of the day, the last train to Chengdu left Xi'an North just before 8 p.m.

Which stations
In Xi'an
Xi'an North (西安北) handles almost all high-speed trains to Chengdu. It is on Metro Lines 2, 4 and 14. Line 2 runs south into the walled city to the Bell Tower (钟楼), and Line 14 runs to Xianyang International Airport.
A search for Xi'an on 12306 also shows two other stations. Several high-speed trains to Chengdu start at Xi'an East (西安东) , a new station, and reach Xi'an North about 25 minutes later. Board at whichever is closer to your hotel; the fare from Xi'an East is a little higher. Xi'an Station (西安) only has slow trains to Chengdu, taking about 9 to 15 hours.
In Chengdu
Chengdu East (成都东) is where the Xi'an trains arrive. Every high-speed train from Xi'an North stopped there on the date checked, and an occasional train also calls at Chengdu South (成都南). Chengdu East is on Metro Lines 2 and 7, and Line 2 runs west to Chunxi Road in six stops and Tianfu Square in seven.
Chengdu West (成都西) only sees the slow conventional trains from Xi'an, K and Z services taking roughly 11 to 15 hours.
Train or plane?
Flying rarely saves time on this route. Trip.com's route page, based on its data for the previous four weeks, listed about 22 flights a week from Xi'an to Chengdu, against several trains an hour, with a flight time of about 1 hour 40 minutes. Most flights land at Chengdu Tianfu International Airport. Once you add the trips to and from both airports, check-in and security, the door-to-door time is usually no shorter than the train's 3.5 to 4 hours from station to station.
A flight makes sense if you are connecting to another flight, or if the trains you want are sold out over a holiday. Xianyang Airport is on Xi'an Metro Line 14, the same line that serves Xi'an North. Tianfu Airport is on Chengdu Metro Line 18, which starts at Chengdu South (火车南站). For booking domestic flights with a foreign passport, see our domestic flights guide .
Breaking the journey
Two stops on the line make good breaks if you have a spare day. Buy two separate tickets on 12306, one to the stop and one onward.
Hanzhong (汉中)
Nearly every train from Xi'an North to Chengdu East stops here. From Xi'an North the ride takes 1 hour 9 minutes to 1 hour 45 minutes (¥78–116 in second class), and from Hanzhong to Chengdu East 2 hours 10 minutes to 2 hours 53 minutes (¥134–200).
The city government describes Hanzhong as the birthplace of the Han: Liu Bang held it as King of Han before he founded the Han dynasty, and the name passed to the Han people and the Chinese language. In spring the plain around the city turns yellow with rapeseed flowers; the 2026 flower festival ran from 18 March to mid-April.
Jianmen Pass (剑门关)
Only a handful of trains a day stop at Jianmenguan station. From Xi'an North they take 2 hours 23 minutes to 2 hours 32 minutes (¥171–191), and from Jianmenguan to Chengdu East 1 hour 14 minutes to 1 hour 57 minutes (¥98–115).
The pass guarded the old road from the north into Sichuan, and Li Bai wrote about it in his poem on the hard road to Shu (蜀道难). A shuttle bus runs from the station to the scenic area's North Gate visitor center and South Gate about every half hour from 8:00 to 19:30, according to the Jiange County transport bureau.
Booking and boarding
Tickets go on sale 15 days ahead, counting the day of travel: at 10:30 Beijing time for trains from Xi'an North, and at 08:45 for trains from Chengdu East.
On ordinary days a day or two ahead is usually enough. On 27 September, almost nine in ten trains from Xi'an North to Chengdu East still had second-class seats for Tuesday 29 September. Holidays are another matter. For Thursday 1 October, the first day of the National Day holiday, second class was sold out on almost nine in ten trains four days ahead, and for 7 October from Chengdu East to Xi'an North on nearly three in four.
Book on the official 12306 English site or app with your passport, through an agency, or at a station ticket window. Our 12306 guide and guide to buying high-speed rail tickets cover the steps and fees.
Your passport is the ticket, so carry the one you booked with and show it at the entrance, the ticket gate and the exit. Some automatic gates read foreign passports; if yours does not, use the staffed lane (人工通道). Ticket checks close shortly before departure, at a time each station announces.
