Hello. Can you help me find a guide to show me around Beijing and accompany me to the rural villages? Male or female is good with me. Please let me know at earliest convenience. Thanks.

2025-09-25

Hello. According to China's "Regulations on the Administration of Tour Guides" (导游人员管理条例) and other relevant laws and regulations, tour guides must be assigned by travel agencies in order to provide guiding services.
You may directly contact a travel agency to arrange your itinerary. Information on travel agencies can be found on the "Beijing Tourism" website:
https://english.visitbeijing.com.cn/service/travelagency
Thank you for your understanding and support.
Beijing Municipal Bureau of Culture and Tourism
