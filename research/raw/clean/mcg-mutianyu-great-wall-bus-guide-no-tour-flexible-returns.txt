Mutianyu by Public Bus: Plan the Return First

I have done this run six times in two years, alone and with family. The bus part is easy. The part worth planning is the return, because the last minibus out of Mutianyu leaves around 16:30 and the last 916 Express from Huairou goes at about 19:30 in summer, 18:30 in winter.
Work backwards from those two numbers and the whole day arranges itself. Get them wrong and you are paying ¥300 for a Didi back to Beijing, which is still cheaper than the tour you skipped.
Beijing to Huairou: the 916 Express
From Dongzhimen Transport Hub, signed 东直门枢纽站 from Exit B or E, one level up. Subway Lines 2 and 13 and the Airport Express all reach it. Look for the board showing 916快 to 怀柔汽车站.
¥12 tapping in with a Yikatong card or the Alipay/WeChat transit QR — tap again on the way out. Cash is about ¥16 and drivers do not give change, so tap.

No Yikatong? Add the 北京一卡通 mini-programme inside Alipay before you board; it works immediately ( card versus phone on Beijing transit ).

1h15 off-peak, 1h45 to 2h at weekends and rush hour, mostly at the expressway toll gates.

Seats fill about ten minutes before departure. Board by 07:30 if you want one.

Get off at Yujiayuan (于家园) or Beidajie (北大街), not the final stop. Both are inside Huairou town and both put you at the transfer point.

Drivers will approach you the moment you step off the 916 holding handwritten Mutianyu signs. That is normal rather than a scam — just agree the number, and agree whether they wait, before you sit down.
For two people the bus-plus-taxi combination is almost always the right balance. For four, chartering a car for the day at ¥600–900 works out competitive and saves the transfers.

The combination most people want is entry, the shuttle, the cable car up and the toboggan down: ¥255 all in. Bring the physical passport — the gate scans it and matches it to the ticket, and a photo on your phone is not accepted. On weekends and in Golden Week, book entry in advance through the 慕田峪长城 WeChat mini-programme; walk-up is not guaranteed and the cable-car queue can run 45 to 90 minutes.
Getting back, which is the actual plan
The wall closes at 17:00 in summer and 16:30 in winter, and the cable car stops selling up-tickets 30 minutes before that.
A realistic late timeline: last cable car down 16:30, taxi into Huairou by 17:15, 916 Express by 17:30, Dongzhimen around 19:00. That works. Relying on the H-line minibus instead does not — its last departure is at roughly the same moment the cable car is coming down.
If you miss the 916 anyway: a Didi from Mutianyu into central Beijing is ¥280–360 and 1h45 to 2h30 depending on traffic. Or stay in Huairou, ¥180–300 on Trip.com, and take the first bus in the morning.

Three things from six trips
Go on a Tuesday or Wednesday. Weekends bring domestic coach tours and the wall feels different.

Amap shows live 916 arrival times and navigates the Huairou transfer, which the signage does not.

Carry ¥200 cash. Rural drivers occasionally have no signal for mobile payment, and that is a bad moment to discover it.

FAQ
Is there a direct bus from Beijing to the Mutianyu gate?
No. Every legitimate route involves one transfer in Huairou. Anything advertised as a direct no-tour shuttle is either a paid tour product or a seasonal service that may not run.
How much does Mutianyu cost without a tour?
About ¥320 to ¥385 per person: ¥65 to ¥130 of transport depending on whether you take the minibus or a taxi from Huairou, plus ¥255 for entry, the compulsory shuttle, the cable car up and the toboggan down.
What is the latest I can arrive and still walk the wall?
The ticket window shuts at 16:00 in summer and 15:30 in winter. You need to be through the gate by then, on the shuttle by 16:15, and on the last cable car up shortly after.
Do I need my passport at Mutianyu?
Yes, the physical one. Entry is real-name ticketed and the gate scans the passport against your ticket. A photo on your phone will not do.
