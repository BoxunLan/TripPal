The high-speed train is the simplest way between Chengdu and Chongqing. The fastest trains take 62 minutes from Chengdu East to Shapingba, most fast trains take about an hour and a half, and well over 100 trains run in each direction every day. On the dates checked for this guide, second class on trains taking under 1 hour 45 minutes cost ¥121 to ¥175. Outside public holidays, you can usually buy a day or two before you travel.
The harder choice is the station. In Chengdu, most trains, including all of the fastest, leave from Chengdu East. Chongqing has three stations you are likely to use, Shapingba, Chongqing West and Chongqing North, in different parts of a large, hilly city. Choose the Chongqing station by where you are staying, then choose the train.
Times, fares and seat availability below come from China Railway's 12306 booking system, searched on 27 September 2026 for travel on Tuesday 29 September and Friday 9 October 2026. Fares differ from train to train.

The return trip mirrors this: about as many trains run back to Chengdu, nearly all of them to Chengdu East, and the fastest again take 62 minutes from Shapingba. On the day checked, trains from Chengdu East to Chongqing ran from just before 6 a.m. until just after 10 p.m. Toward Chengdu East, the first train left Chongqing North at about 5:30 a.m. and the last fast train left Chongqing West just before 10 p.m.; a later D train from Chongqing North arrived after 1 a.m.
Sort the 12306 results by travel time before you buy. A few long-distance trains that happen to link the two cities take the long way round, for example via Zigong and Luzhou, or through Guizhou. They take longer and cost more.

Which Chengdu station
Chengdu East (成都东) is the station for this trip. Most trains to Chongqing leave from here, including all of the fastest. It is on Metro Lines 2 and 7.
Chengdu South (成都南) has far fewer trains to Chongqing. It is on Metro Lines 1, 7 and 18, which can save a cross-town ride if you are staying in the south of the city.
Chengdu West (成都西) handles the slow conventional trains: K, T and Z services that took 2 hours 48 minutes to 5 hours 26 minutes on 29 September. It is on Metro Lines 4 and 9.
Chengdu Station (成都站) , which locals still call the North Station (火车北站), is closed. It stopped handling passengers on 11 October 2022 for a rebuild, and its main services moved to Chengdu West. People's Daily Online reported in June 2026 that the new station is due to open in 2027, together with a new high-speed line to Chongqing.
Which Chongqing station
Shapingba (沙坪坝) is the fastest option: one or two G trains an hour from Chengdu East, the quickest in 62 minutes. Metro Lines 1, 9 and the Loop Line meet here, joined by an underground transfer hall. Line 1 runs east without a change to Jiaochangkou, in the Jiefangbei area, and on to Xiaoshizi.
Chongqing West (重庆西) has the most trains: several an hour from Chengdu East, most of them G trains taking under 1 hour 45 minutes. It is on the Loop Line and Metro Line 5.
Chongqing North (重庆北) also gets several trains an hour from Chengdu East. Most are D trains that take around 2 hours 15 minutes but cost less, ¥85 to ¥113 in second class on the dates checked. Its G trains cost about the same as those to Chongqing West. The station has two entrances, North Square (北广场) and South Square (南广场), linked inside the station since January 2021. Metro Line 10 serves both; North Square also has Line 4, and South Square has Line 3 and the Loop Line.
Two other stops also appear in search results. Chongqing East (重庆东) opened in June 2025 on Metro Line 6 and gets a few trains a day from Chengdu East, taking about 2 hours. Some trains also continue to Jiangbei Airport station (江北机场).
How much it costs
Each train has its own fare, and on this route the spread is wide. Second class on the dates checked:
Chengdu East to Shapingba: ¥139–164.

Chengdu East to Chongqing West, on trains under 1 hour 45 minutes: ¥121–172.

Chengdu East to Chongqing North, on D trains: ¥85–113.

Chengdu West to Chongqing North, on conventional trains: from ¥50.50 for a hard seat.

First class cost ¥200–275 on the fast trains to Shapingba and Chongqing West, and business class, sold on most G trains, cost ¥378–601. Fares in the other direction fell in the same ranges.
If seats are gone, 12306 often still sells standing tickets (无座, "no seat") at the second-class price. For a trip of about an hour, that is a workable fallback.
Do you need to book ahead?
On an ordinary weekday, not far ahead. On 27 September, second-class seats were still on sale on almost nine in ten G and D trains between Chengdu East and the three main Chongqing stations for Tuesday 29 September, and on nearly all of them for Friday 9 October.
Public holidays are different. For Thursday 1 October 2026, the first day of the National Day holiday, second class was already sold out on three in four of those trains four days ahead. For the trip back to Chengdu on 7 October, it was sold out on about two in three. If you travel in a holiday week, book as soon as sales open.
Tickets go on sale 15 days ahead, counting the day of travel, at a fixed time for each departure station (Beijing time): 08:45 at Chengdu East, 09:00 at Chengdu South and Chengdu West, and 11:00 at Shapingba, Chongqing West, Chongqing North and Chongqing East.
Getting into the city
Chengdu
Chengdu East: Metro Lines 2 and 7. Line 2 runs west to Chunxi Road in six stops and Tianfu Square in seven.

Chengdu South: Metro Lines 1, 7 and 18.

Chengdu West: Metro Lines 4 and 9.

For neighborhoods and what to see, read our Chengdu travel guide .
Chongqing
Shapingba: Metro Lines 1, 9 and the Loop Line. Line 1 goes straight to Jiaochangkou and Xiaoshizi.

Chongqing West: the Loop Line and Line 5.

Chongqing North: Line 10 at both squares, Line 4 at North Square, and Line 3 and the Loop Line at South Square.

Chongqing East: Line 6.

Buying tickets and boarding with a foreign passport
You can book on the official 12306 English website or app with your passport, through an agency such as Trip.com, or at a ticket window in the station. Our 12306 guide for foreigners covers registration and passport details, and how to buy China high-speed rail tickets compares the channels and their fees.
Your passport is your ticket. China Railway issues e-tickets, and you show the passport you booked with to enter the station, to pass the ticket gate and to leave at the other end. A screenshot or a printed itinerary is not a ticket.
Expect a security check at the entrance. Some automatic ticket gates read foreign passports; if yours is rejected, use the staffed lane (人工通道). Ticket checks close shortly before departure, at a time each station announces, so allow time for both queues.
