Hello! I would like to ask the matter of purchasing real estate in China. I couldn’t find your contact number.

2025-07-22

Hello.

Foreign individuals holding a work visa or journalist visa and working within the Chinese mainland are allowed to purchase one residential property in Beijing for self-use. At the time of purchase, the buyer must be physically present in the Chinese mainland and must complete temporary residence registration with the public security authorities.

Specific steps:
Go to the official website of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources → Homepage (首页) → Government Services (政务服务) → Beijing Online Real Estate Registration Service Platform (北京市不动产登记领域网上办事服务平台) → Business Handling (业务办理) → Personal Home Purchase Qualification Review (个人购房资格审核) → Beijing Home Purchase Eligibility Verification Platform (北京市购房资格核验平台) . Once registered, you can submit your application for eligibility review.
Website: https://ghzrzyw.beijing.gov.cn/

For inquiries about home purchase policies for foreign nationals in Beijing, please call +86-010-55598501 or +86-010-55597026 (Note: currently no foreign language service is available).

Thank you for your understanding and support of our work!

Beijing Municipal Commission of Housing and Urban-rural Development
