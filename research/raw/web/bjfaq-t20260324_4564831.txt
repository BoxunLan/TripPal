If a foreigner renews his passport, can he still use the self-service clearance channel at Beijing's airports to enter China with the new passport and a valid "Five-Star Card"?

2026-03-24

Hello. If the passport number changes after renewal, the holder cannot use the self-service immigration clearance channel when entering the Chinese mainland for the first time with the new passport. He/she must use the manual inspection channel to complete biometric information registration for self-service clearance. After the registration is completed, he/she may use the self-service channel for future entries.
For more detailed information, you may call the National Immigration Administration hotline at +86-10-12367 (foreign language services are available).
Thank you for your understanding and support.
