For London to Shanghai in 2026, British Airways wins on schedule consistency, Executive Club earnings, and Oneworld connection options, while China Eastern wins on price (roughly £100-£250 cheaper in economy), onboard Chinese food, and seamless domestic China connections through Pudong. Economy returns cluster at £500-£800 on BA and £450-£700 on China Eastern when booked 8-12 weeks ahead, with both flying daytime Heathrow departures that land in Shanghai the following morning.

2026 Schedules and Routing
Both carriers fly nonstop between London Heathrow (LHR) and Shanghai Pudong (PVG). Neither airline serves Hongqiao (SHA) from London — SHA is a domestic and regional airport with only a handful of short-haul international routes. If your final destination in China is not Shanghai, you'll either transit at PVG or take a domestic HSR onward.
British Airways LHR-PVG
BA operates the route with Boeing 787-9 aircraft. Typical 2026 schedule is 4-5 weekly departures (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat, Sun in most months), climbing to daily service during summer peak. Westbound departure is a mid-morning Heathrow push with an early-next-morning PVG arrival, roughly 11 hours 15 minutes in the air. Flight number BA169 outbound, BA168 return.
China Eastern LHR-PVG
China Eastern also uses the 787-9 on the LHR-PVG rotation, with 3-4 weekly departures in 2026 (typically Mon, Thu, Fri, Sun). Departure from Heathrow is typically early afternoon, arriving PVG the next morning, roughly 11 hours 30 minutes airborne. Flight number MU552 outbound, MU551 return.
For both airlines, eastbound tailwinds make the return London flight around 30-60 minutes shorter than the outbound.

Economy Price Comparison (2026)
Booking 8-12 weeks ahead for shoulder-season travel (April, May, September, October), expect these economy return bands on LHR-PVG:
British Airways economy : £500-£800 return for Euro Traveller fares, £720-£1,050 for flexible World Traveller Plus downgrades and fuller Y fares.

China Eastern economy : £450-£700 return for standard economy, £590-£820 for flexible fares.

During peak summer (July-August) and winter holiday weeks (late December), both airlines climb £100-£250 above these bands. Chinese New Year (late January/early February 2026) pushes economy over £900 on both carriers.
Premium Economy
Only BA offers a genuine premium economy cabin on this route (World Traveller Plus, 38 inches of pitch, wider seats, dedicated meals). China Eastern's "Premium Economy" label in 2026 is effectively extra-legroom economy with 35-36 inches of pitch and the same meal service. BA's World Traveller Plus typically runs £1,400-£1,900 return; China Eastern's equivalent row runs £850-£1,200.
Business Class
BA Club World : fully flat beds on 787-9 Club Suite, direct aisle access, polished LHR T5 Galleries First lounge, typical 2026 returns £3,800-£5,200.

China Eastern Business : fully flat seats in a 1-2-1 configuration on 787-9, Shanghai premium lounge at PVG, typical 2026 returns £2,800-£3,900.

China Eastern Business is the clear value play if you want lie-flat on a long-haul without £4k outlay. BA Club World wins on LHR-side lounge, service consistency, and the polish of British cabin crew training.
Onboard Experience
Seat and cabin
Both airlines fly 787-9s in similar 3-3-3 economy configurations. BA's Club Suite business cabin is newer and more consistently refurbished than China Eastern's mixed 1-2-1 business cabin, which can vary between older and newer deliveries depending on which frame you draw. Economy pitch on both airlines sits at 31 inches for BA and 30-31 inches for China Eastern.
Meals
BA Euro Traveller economy serves two hot meals on LHR-PVG: typically a hot dinner shortly after takeoff (chicken or pasta, bread roll, dessert, tea/coffee) and a lighter pre-arrival breakfast (omelette or congee option). Alcohol is complimentary: house wine, beer, and spirits.
China Eastern serves two hot meals as well, with a stronger Chinese menu rotation — Sichuan-style chicken, mushroom rice, steamed fish, and a rice-porridge pre-arrival breakfast. Western options are always available but the Chinese mains are noticeably better than what BA carries. Alcohol is complimentary on international long-haul but the wine selection in economy is thinner.
In-flight entertainment
Both airlines run seatback IFE with a comparable 300-500 hour movie library. BA's system is English-first with Chinese subtitles on a subset of films. China Eastern's system is Chinese-first with strong English UI and a heavier rotation of Mandarin and Cantonese films plus Hollywood new releases. Screen sizes are similar (12-inch economy, larger in premium).
Wi-Fi
BA charges roughly £12.99 for full-flight Wi-Fi in economy (streaming-capable passes cost more). China Eastern charges roughly ¥99 (around £11) for full-flight access. Neither is free in economy. Download Netflix, Spotify, and offline maps before boarding.

Baggage Allowance (2026)
BA Euro Traveller economy on long-haul routes like LHR-PVG includes 1 checked bag of 23 kg plus 1 cabin bag up to 23 kg plus a personal item. Two checked bags require a World Traveller Plus fare or paid upgrade.
China Eastern economy on international long-haul includes 2 checked bags of 23 kg each plus 1 cabin bag of 8 kg plus a personal item. The doubled checked allowance is meaningful if you're planning to shop in Shanghai or buy tea in Hangzhou and bring it home.
Loyalty: Executive Club vs SkyTeam Elite
BA Executive Club (Oneworld)
Earning Tier Points and Avios on BA-operated flights is straightforward. LHR-PVG return in economy at a full Y or B booking class earns around 280-560 Tier Points and 6,000-9,500 Avios depending on fare class. BA Silver (Oneworld Sapphire) lounge access applies at LHR T5 Galleries South and the Cathay Pacific lounge at PVG Terminal 2.
China Eastern (SkyTeam)
LHR-PVG return earns 25-100 percent base miles depending on fare class into the Eastern Miles program, transferable within SkyTeam partners including Air France-KLM's Flying Blue, Delta SkyMiles, and Korean Air SKYPASS. SkyTeam Elite Plus (Air France Silver or Delta Platinum and above) gets lounge access at PVG and extra baggage.
If you already hold status on BA, KLM, Delta, or American Airlines, let that drive the choice more than raw fare. A £150 savings on China Eastern is easily erased by losing Executive Club status points or access to T5's Concorde Room.
Reliability
Based on publicly reported on-time performance data through 2025-2026 on the LHR-PVG corridor: British Airways typically posts on-time arrival rates around 78-82 percent, China Eastern around 72-77 percent. Both fall in the industry-normal range for long-haul widebody operations out of Heathrow, where slot constraints and winter weather routinely cause 15-30 minute pushbacks regardless of carrier.
Baggage handling accuracy is broadly comparable with BA edging a percentage point or two ahead on mishandled baggage reports per thousand passengers. If you're checking anything fragile or valuable, pack it in your carry-on regardless of airline.
Who Should Fly Which
Fly British Airways if
You hold or are building Executive Club status, or fly regularly on Oneworld (AA, Qatar, Cathay, JAL)

You want the cleanest LHR-side experience: T5 Galleries lounge, reliable Euro Traveller check-in flow, English-speaking ground staff

Schedule consistency matters more than £100-£200 of ticket savings

You're connecting within Europe on a BA or partner short-haul that same day

Fly China Eastern if
Budget is the deciding factor and you want the cheapest LHR-PVG return

You want better Chinese food in economy

You have onward domestic China flights and want a single PNR through China Eastern's massive Chinese network (Chengdu, Kunming, Xi'an, Guangzhou, Xiamen)

Two checked bags of 23 kg in economy matter to you

You already hold SkyTeam status through Air France, KLM, Delta, or Korean Air

Booking Tips
Book 8-12 weeks ahead for the best economy fares. Neither airline discounts inside 14 days for this route.

Compare on Google Flights or Skyscanner then price the same itinerary directly on the airline's website. BA often beats third-party retailers by £10-£30, and China Eastern sometimes offers direct-booking promos.

Check PVG terminal on your e-ticket. Both airlines use PVG Terminal 2 in 2026 but schedule changes happen.

Download both airline apps before flying. BA's app shows real-time gate changes and mobile boarding passes. China Eastern's app integrates with Alipay for QR boarding, and the Mandarin interface has an English toggle in settings.

Budget for PVG-to-city transfer : Maglev + Metro runs about ¥55 to the Bund area, Didi to most central Shanghai hotels is ¥180-260.

Frequently Asked Questions
Which is cheaper in 2026, BA or China Eastern to Shanghai?
China Eastern is typically £100-£250 cheaper in economy for comparable dates booked 8-12 weeks ahead. Business class shows an even wider gap of £700-£1,200 in China Eastern's favor.
Does BA fly to Shanghai Hongqiao?
No. Neither BA nor China Eastern flies LHR to Hongqiao (SHA). All London nonstops land at Pudong (PVG). Hongqiao is primarily a domestic and regional airport.
Can I use Alipay to book a China Eastern ticket from the UK?
Yes, on the China Eastern website. BA's UK site accepts Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and PayPal but not Alipay. Most UK travelers pay on a home credit card regardless to earn airline-card bonus miles.
Is China Eastern safe?
China Eastern Airlines is an IOSA-certified SkyTeam member and operates modern 787-9, A350, and A330 aircraft on long-haul routes. Its safety record since 2022 has been in line with other major international carriers.
Which airline has better food on the London-Shanghai route?
China Eastern has stronger Chinese main courses in economy, which is a material difference on a flight where you're eating two hot meals. BA's service feels more polished in business class with better wine selection and brand-name amenity kits.
Can I connect to a domestic Chinese city on the same ticket?
On China Eastern, yes — PVG is their international hub and the airline sells throughfares from London to Chengdu, Xi'an, Kunming, Guangzhou, and dozens of other domestic cities on a single PNR with bags checked through. On British Airways, domestic Chinese connections are limited to codeshares with Qatar Airways and partners, which in practice rarely beats simply flying BA to Shanghai and booking a separate HSR or domestic flight onward.
Does either airline offer stopovers in the UK or China?
BA's fare rules occasionally allow a stopover in London on multi-stop itineraries if you're booking a round-the-world Oneworld fare. China Eastern does not offer a formal Shanghai stopover product for short-term tourists, though the 240-hour (10-day) visa-free transit policy effectively lets any China Eastern or BA passenger spend up to 10 days in Shanghai on a transit basis as long as they're continuing to a third country.
Arrival at PVG and Onward to Central Shanghai
Both BA and China Eastern arrive at Pudong (PVG) Terminal 2 in 2026. Immigration and baggage typically take 30-60 minutes at peak arrival times. Once cleared, you have four main ways to reach central Shanghai, in rough order of speed:
Maglev train plus Metro Line 2 — the Shanghai Maglev , which has run at 300 km/h on every departure since May 1, 2020, hits Longyang Road in 8 minutes (¥50 single, ¥40 with same-day boarding pass). Transfer to Metro Line 2 to Lujiazui, Nanjing East Road, or People's Square. Total PVG to the Bund in around 40 minutes, ¥55 all-in.

Metro Line 2 direct — No Maglev, no transfer. Slower (70-80 minutes to Nanjing East Road) but cheaper at ¥8 one-way.

Didi or taxi — ¥180-260 for most central hotels, 50-90 minutes depending on traffic. Book Didi through the app using your Alipay or WeChat Pay link.

Airport shuttle bus — Lines 1-7 run from PVG to fixed downtown drop-offs, ¥22-32 one-way. Slower than rail but useful if you're heading to a hotel that's neither near a subway nor worth a Didi.

Download both airlines' apps before arrival, confirm your e-ticket on screen, and have your Alipay QR ready. Shanghai immigration officers in 2026 no longer require paper arrival cards for visa-free transit passengers — everything is handled at the passport desk.
