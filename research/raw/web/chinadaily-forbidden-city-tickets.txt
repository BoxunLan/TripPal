How to buy advance general admission tickets for the Forbidden City?

You can buy advance tickets for the Forbidden City (Palace Museum) in Beijing online through the official website or via authorized platforms. Here’s how:

1. Official Website (Recommended)
• English website: https://bookingticket.dpm.org.cn/
• Steps:
1. Create an account using your email address and passport number.
2. Select your preferred date and time slot.
3. Complete payment using Alipay, WeChat Pay, or a Chinese bank card.
4. Show your passport at the entrance—no need for a physical ticket.

2. Third-Party Platforms (For English Users)
If you don’t read Chinese, you can book through platforms like:
• Trip.com (https://www.trip.com)
• Klook (https://www.klook.com)
These sites allow payment with international credit cards and offer customer support in English.

Important Tips:
• Tickets sell out fast (especially on weekends and holidays), so book at least a few days in advance.
• No on-site ticket sales—all tickets must be purchased online.
• Bring your passport, as it serves as your entry pass.
