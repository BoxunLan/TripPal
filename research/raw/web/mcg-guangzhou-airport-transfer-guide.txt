Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN) is 28 km north of central Guangzhou, and getting into the city is cheap and easy if you know which counter to head for. Metro Line 3 runs right into the airport for ¥16-19 and drops you at Tiyu Xilu or Zhujiang New Town in around 50 minutes. Airport shuttle buses cover the hotels in Tianhe, Yuexiu, and Pazhou for ¥20-32. A taxi is ¥100-150 to most downtown addresses, and Didi is usually a bit cheaper. The Pearl River Delta connections, for onward travel to Shenzhen, Zhuhai, or Hong Kong, have gotten faster in 2026. This guide covers every option with 2026 pricing and the practical details nobody tells you at baggage claim.

Baiyun Airport Layout
Guangzhou Baiyun Airport operates two main terminals. Your arrival terminal changes the walking path to transport, so check your boarding pass or airline app.
Terminal 1 (T1)
T1 handles most domestic flights on Air China, Sichuan, Shandong, and several international carriers. The ground transportation center (GTC) sits directly in front of T1, with the metro station entrance in the basement of the GTC.
Terminal 2 (T2)
T2 is newer and handles China Southern domestic and most international flights, including routes from Europe, North America, Southeast Asia, and Oceania. T2 connects to its own GTC and has a direct link to the metro station on its lower level.
Walking Between Terminals
If you land at one terminal and need transport from the other, a free shuttle bus runs every 10-15 minutes between T1 and T2 at ground transportation level. An underground walkway also connects the two, taking 8-12 minutes on foot.
Signage and English Support
Airport signage is bilingual English/Chinese throughout. Staff at the information counters (marked with a blue "i") generally handle basic English. Metro station signs, ticket machines, and train announcements all have English versions.
Metro Line 3: The Cheapest Way Into Guangzhou
Guangzhou Metro Line 3 North extension terminates at Airport South (T1) and Airport North (T2), and it is the cheapest, most predictable transfer into the city.
Route and Key Stops
From the airport, Line 3 heads south and stops at:
Airport North (T2) or Airport South (T1)

Renhe

Longgui

Jiahewanggang (transfer to Line 2 and Line 14)

Baiyun Park

Yantang

Tianhe Coach Terminal (transfer to Line 6)

Linhexi (transfer to Guangzhou East Railway Station)

Tiyu Xilu (shopping, business hotels)

Shipaiqiao

Zhujiang New Town (CBD, Canton Tower, Grand Hyatt, Four Seasons)

Fares and Travel Time
Baiyun Airport Metro Line 3 fares in 2026:
Airport to Tiyu Xilu: ¥16, about 45-50 minutes

Airport to Zhujiang New Town: ¥16, about 50-55 minutes

Airport to Guangzhou Railway Station (via transfer at Jiahewanggang to Line 2): ¥17-19, 55-65 minutes

Airport to Guangzhou South Railway Station (HSR hub, via Line 2 transfer): ¥18-19, 75-90 minutes

How to Pay
The easiest way in 2026 is scanning with Alipay or WeChat Pay. Open either app, find "Metro QR" (Chengshi Ditie Yikatong) for Guangzhou, and tap through the turnstiles. Your ride deducts automatically from the linked wallet.
Backup options: a Lingnan Pass (Guangzhou's physical stored-value card, refundable at any station), single-ride paper tokens from the ticket machines (select English, pay cash), or a direct QR purchase at the vending machine kiosk.
Operating Hours
First trains leave Baiyun Airport around 06:10 and the last train south departs around 23:00 in 2026. If you land after 23:00, metro is not an option; see the taxi or Didi sections below.
Airport Shuttle Buses
Eleven to fourteen shuttle bus routes fan out from Baiyun Airport into central Guangzhou and nearby cities. They are cheaper than a taxi for two people traveling together, more comfortable than metro if you have heavy bags, and drop you at major hotels or transit hubs.
Main City Lines
Line 1: Airport to Guangzhou Railway Station via Tianhe district, ¥22

Line 2: Airport to Guangzhou East Railway Station, ¥22

Line 3: Airport to Fangcun (western Guangzhou) via Pazhou, ¥26

Line 5: Airport to Guangzhou South Railway Station, ¥32

Line 6: Airport to Zhujiang New Town (CBD), ¥22

Pickup Locations
All shuttle buses depart from the Ground Transportation Center at both T1 and T2. Signs in English point to "Airport Shuttle Bus." Counters at the bus hall sell tickets in cash or via Alipay and WeChat Pay; some lines accept contactless bank cards.
Frequency and Hours
Most lines run every 20-40 minutes from 06:30 to 23:00, with a few overnight lines operating at reduced frequency. Journey times vary from 45 to 90 minutes depending on traffic.
When to Choose the Shuttle Bus
You have large bags and do not want to wrestle them on the metro.

You are heading to a hotel near Tianhe or Zhujiang New Town and prefer a seated ride.

You are arriving late evening when metro is still running but you want to sit down.

Your budget sits above metro but below taxi.

Taxi: Straightforward but More Expensive
Taxis are plentiful, regulated, and safer than random drivers who approach you inside the terminal.
Fare and Time
Baiyun Airport to Tiyu Xilu: ¥100-130, about 40 minutes off-peak

Baiyun Airport to Zhujiang New Town: ¥110-140, 40-55 minutes

Baiyun Airport to Guangzhou South Railway Station: ¥130-170, 55-75 minutes

Baiyun Airport to Foshan downtown: ¥150-220, 60-80 minutes

Where to Queue
At both T1 and T2, the official taxi queue is marked with English signage at the ground transportation level, just outside the arrivals hall. Taxis wait in a single line, passengers take the next car, and a staff member helps with English addresses if needed.
How to Pay
Every official Guangzhou taxi accepts Alipay and WeChat Pay QR codes mounted on the dashboard. Cash works. Credit card acceptance is still rare.
Scam Avoidance
Use the official taxi queue, never accept a ride from anyone who approaches you inside the terminal.

Confirm the driver starts the meter (da biao) before pulling away.

Keep your address ready in Chinese characters; show it on your phone or a card.

The meter starts at ¥12 for the first 2.5 km and roughly ¥2.6 per km after. An airport surcharge of ¥1 and a highway toll of ¥15-30 are added on top.

Didi Rideshare: Usually a Small Saving
Didi in 2026 has a complete English interface and accepts international credit cards linked through Alipay or directly. For most airport rides, Didi saves 10-20 percent versus a metered taxi, though prices surge during peak hours and heavy rain.
How to Book
Open the Didi app, set pickup to "Baiyun Airport Terminal 1" or "Terminal 2" (the app auto-detects the designated rideshare pickup zone).

Enter your destination in Chinese characters or pinyin.

Choose Didi Express (cheapest), Premier (slightly better cars), or Didi Taxi (uses a regular taxi with the app dispatch).

Confirm and walk to the designated pickup area marked with English signs.

Fare Ranges
Didi Express to Tiyu Xilu: ¥90-120

Didi Express to Zhujiang New Town: ¥100-135

Didi Express to Guangzhou South: ¥120-160

Didi Premier: add 20-30 percent

Payment
Didi processes payment automatically at ride end. Funds come from Alipay or WeChat Pay balance, or from the international card linked in the app. Tipping is not standard and should not be expected.

Onward to Shenzhen, Zhuhai, and Hong Kong
Guangzhou is the central hub of the Pearl River Delta, and Baiyun Airport has solid onward links to nearby cities.
Shenzhen
HSR from Guangzhou South (G and D trains): 30-45 min, ¥75-120 second class. Reach Guangzhou South from the airport via Metro Line 3 + Line 2, or direct shuttle bus Line 5.

Intercity bus from Baiyun Airport directly to Shenzhen: ¥120-160, 2h 30 to 3h. Buses leave every 30-60 minutes during the day.

Direct from Guangzhou East to Shenzhen: D-class trains ¥80-100, around 1 hour.

Zhuhai
HSR from Guangzhou South to Zhuhai on the intercity line: 55-80 min, ¥75-100.

Airport express bus Baiyun to Zhuhai: ¥130-170, 3-3.5 hours. Less convenient than combining metro and HSR.

Hong Kong
HSR from Guangzhou South to West Kowloon: 48-70 min, ¥215-260 second class. You will need a valid Hong Kong entry stamp; most passport holders get 7-90 days on arrival. Bring your passport; security plus immigration at the HSR station takes 30-45 minutes.

Bus: Several coach companies run direct Baiyun Airport to Hong Kong Airport routes, 3.5-4 hours, ¥180-280. Useful if your ticket is split and you need to reach HKIA for a second flight.

Foshan
Metro Line 3 transfer at Xilang or Linhexi to Guangfo Line: 60-90 min, ¥8-14.

Taxi: ¥150-220, 60-80 min.

Apps and Payment Setup in 2026
Before you land, install and verify these apps. Airport Wi-Fi is free but verification delays can eat into your connection time.
Core Apps
Alipay (Zhifubao): Add an international credit card. Use for metro QR, taxis, restaurants, and Didi payment.

WeChat Pay: Same as Alipay, second option for redundancy, also used for messaging Chinese contacts.

Amap (Gaode): The best map and navigation app in China. Full English interface. Shows metro times, bus routes, and walking directions inside Baiyun Airport.

Didi: Rideshare with full English support in 2026.

12306: Rail backup for HSR to Shenzhen, Zhuhai, or Hong Kong.

Meituan: Food delivery and restaurant booking. Useful once you are in your hotel.

Cash Fallback
Bring ¥200-400 in small notes as a fallback. Not every rural bus accepts QR, and a dead phone at a shuttle bus counter is a solvable problem only if you have cash.
FAQ
What is the cheapest way to get from Guangzhou Airport to downtown?
Metro Line 3 is the cheapest option in 2026: ¥16-19 to Tiyu Xilu or Zhujiang New Town in roughly 50 minutes. The metro is fully bilingual, safe, and connects to both T1 (Airport South) and T2 (Airport North) stations directly inside the ground transportation center. Airport shuttle buses (¥22-32) are the next step up for travelers with heavy bags.
How long does it take to get from Baiyun Airport to central Guangzhou?
Metro Line 3 takes 45-55 minutes to Tiyu Xilu or Zhujiang New Town, 55-90 minutes to Guangzhou South Railway Station with a Line 2 transfer. Taxi or Didi rides run 35-55 minutes to downtown in off-peak hours, stretching to 60-75 minutes during morning or evening rush. Airport shuttle buses fall between these, typically 45-90 minutes depending on route and traffic.
Is Didi cheaper than a taxi at Guangzhou Airport?
Usually yes, by about 10-20 percent in 2026. Didi Express to Zhujiang New Town typically runs ¥100-135 while a metered taxi for the same trip is ¥110-140. Surge pricing during heavy rain or rush hour can briefly push Didi above taxi prices. If you want a guaranteed fare, check the Didi estimate in the app before queueing for a taxi.
Does Guangzhou Metro Line 3 run late enough for evening arrivals?
The last southbound trains from Baiyun Airport depart around 23:00 in 2026. If your international flight lands after 22:30 and you need time for immigration and baggage claim, metro is likely not an option. Use a taxi or Didi, or the late-night shuttle bus Line 6A, which runs until about 01:00. First morning trains leave around 06:10.
Can I pay for Guangzhou Airport transport with a foreign credit card?
Directly, no. Guangzhou Metro, shuttle buses, and most Didi rides do not accept foreign cards at the point of sale. The workaround is to link your foreign Visa or Mastercard to Alipay or WeChat Pay through the Tour Pass or International Wallet feature, then pay with the app's QR code. Taxi drivers also prefer Alipay or WeChat Pay over cash in 2026. Keep ¥200-400 cash as a backup for anything that fails.
