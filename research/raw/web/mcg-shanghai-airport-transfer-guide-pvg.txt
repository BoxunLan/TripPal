Shanghai Pudong (PVG) is 40km from the Bund. The good news: there are more ways into the city than any other airport in China. The bad news: the fastest ride and the cheapest ride are not the same thing, and a wrong choice on a jet-lagged night can cost you 90 minutes. This is the real 2026 playbook.

All taxi and Didi prices assume no major traffic. Add ¥40–80 and 20–30 minutes during Friday 5–8pm rush or rain.
Option 1: Shanghai Maglev — the 7-minute ride
The Shanghai Maglev Train (上海磁浮) is the only commercial high-speed maglev in the world. Since May 1, 2020, every departure has run at a top speed of 300 km/h, according to the published timetable. It runs 30km from Pudong Airport Station (under T1/T2) to Longyang Road Station (龙阳路) in Pudong.
The numbers
Economy class: ¥50 one-way, ¥40 with same-day boarding pass, ¥80 return

VIP class: ¥100 one-way

Frequency: every 20 minutes, 07:02 to 21:42 from the airport (06:45 to 21:40 from Longyang Road)

Duration: about 8 minutes at 300 km/h

Top speed: 300 km/h on every departure; the 431 km/h runs were dropped from the timetable on May 1, 2020

Does it actually save time?
Longyang Road is not downtown. You still have to transfer to Metro Line 2 (towards East Nanjing Road/People's Square/Jing'an Temple) for another 20–25 minutes to the city centre. Adding walking between platforms, total door-to-door from PVG to a hotel near the Bund is 45–55 minutes for roughly ¥54.
Verdict: worth it if you're travelling light, you want to see the maglev once in your life, and your hotel is anywhere along Line 2 (Lujiazui, East Nanjing Road, People's Square, Jing'an Temple, West Nanjing Road). Skip it if you're hauling two big suitcases — the platform transfer at Longyang Road involves stairs and escalators.
Where to find it at PVG
Follow "Maglev" (磁浮) signs from arrivals at either T1 or T2. The station sits between the two terminals, connected by covered walkways. Ticket vending machines accept Alipay, WeChat Pay, and cash. The physical ticket is a credit-card-sized plastic card — tap it at the gate, keep it because it doubles as a souvenir.
Option 2: Metro Line 2 direct — the ¥8 budget play
Shanghai Metro Line 2 runs the full east-west spine of the city. The eastern terminus is literally under Pudong Airport T1/T2. The western terminus is Hongqiao Airport T2 and Hongqiao Railway Station.
The numbers
Cost: ¥8 to People's Square, ¥9 to Jing'an Temple or Hongqiao

Duration: 75–90 minutes to the Bund / People's Square

Frequency: every 6–10 minutes

Operating hours: 6:00am to 10:00pm (last train east from Century Avenue at around 10:55pm)

Change at Guanglan Road? No. Line 2 now runs through without a transfer since 2019.

Why it hurts with luggage
Line 2 trains get seriously crowded from Longyang Road westward. Century Avenue and Lujiazui platforms are wall-to-wall humans at rush hour. Two big suitcases will make enemies. Solo travellers with a backpack: go for it — it's the cheapest way downtown by a huge margin.
Ticket options
Buy a single-journey token at a machine (supports Alipay, WeChat Pay, cash), or tap your phone using Metro Daduhui (Shanghai Metro) mini-program inside Alipay. The Shanghai Jiaotong Card (PTC) is the reloadable option if you're staying 3+ days — ¥20 refundable deposit.
Option 3: Airport buses — the forgotten middle ground
PVG has six airport bus lines, but only Line 4 and Line 7 run into the city during the day, and a night line takes over from 23:00. Most travellers ignore them, then regret it once they see the metro line 2 luggage situation.

No daytime airport bus goes to Jing'an Temple, People's Square or the Bund. Line 4 suits hotels around Wujiaochang, Hongkou Football Stadium and Shanghai Railway Station, and Line 7 suits south Shanghai. After 23:00, the night line runs through the city centre along Yan'an Road to Hongqiao.
Buses leave from both T1 and T2; follow the "Airport Bus" signs. Line 4 runs from 07:00 to 23:00 and leaves every 20 to 70 minutes; Line 7 runs from 07:30 to 23:00 and leaves every 25 to 45 minutes. Departures from T2 are a few minutes later than from T1, and the night line runs from 23:00 to 05:30.
Option 4: Metered taxi — the no-brain option
Shanghai taxis are metered, clean, and easy to get. The official taxi rank is on the Arrivals level, signed in English.
The 2026 price
Pudong to the Bund or Jing'an Temple runs ¥180–240 metered:
¥16 flag fall (first 3km)

¥2.5 per km after

¥0.3 per km fuel surcharge

¥20 highway toll (paid by you at the end)

Night surcharge (11pm–5am): +30% on per-km rate

Duration: 45–75 minutes depending on traffic. Friday 5–8pm and rainy days can push 90 minutes and ¥280+.
How to avoid the taxi scam
Only use the official taxi rank — never the men holding signs inside arrivals saying "taxi." Legitimate companies are Dazhong (大众, turquoise), Qiangsheng (强生, yellow), Jinjiang (锦江, white), Bashi (巴士, sky blue). All meter on. Watch the driver press the meter button when you leave the airport. Ask for a printed receipt (发票, fāpiào) — this is your insurance if anything goes wrong, the taxi number is on it.
When taxi wins
Two or more travellers (split two ways, the ¥180–240 fare is ¥90–120 each, more than the ¥54 maglev + metro; with three or four people the gap narrows, and the car takes you to the door)

Heavy luggage

Hotel not within walking distance of a Line 2 station

Late night after 10pm (metro closed)

Rain (airport bus is fine, metro transfers are miserable)

Option 5: Didi Chuxing (the Chinese Uber)
Didi works at PVG. You need the app (download "Didi" on the App Store or Android — international version added in 2023) and a linked international card or Alipay.
Didi pricing tiers in 2026
Didi Express (快车): ¥120–170 PVG to Bund — cheapest

Didi Premier (专车): ¥140–200 — nicer cars, usually black sedans

Didi Luxe (豪华车): ¥260–400 — Audi/BMW/Mercedes, for airport pickups with luggage

Didi requires you to enter the pickup point precisely. At PVG, the designated Didi pickup is the P1/P2 car park, Arrivals level, Column B2 (there's English signage once you're inside the terminal, follow "Ride-hailing" signs). Your driver will WeChat or call you — use in-app translation. Total door-to-door is the same as a taxi (45–75 minutes) but you know the price upfront.
Why Didi wins over taxi
Price locked before you get in — no meter anxiety or "broken meter" scams

In-app English, no communication needed after booking

Electronic receipt and ride history

You pay inside the app, no cash fumbling

Premier cars almost always have bigger boots for two suitcases

Why Didi might lose
You need to set up the app and payment before you land (seriously — do it before your flight)

At peak times the Express tier has a 10–20 minute wait

Taxi rank is often faster if you land on a normal Tuesday afternoon

Option 6: Hotel private transfer / pre-booked cars
Trip.com, Klook, and most 4+ star hotels sell pre-booked transfers for ¥250–450 per car (up to 3 passengers). You get an English-speaking meet-and-greet at arrivals holding a sign, help with luggage, and a Mercedes V-class or similar. Expensive, but if it's your first time in Shanghai and you don't want to deal with a queue, it's genuinely worth it for one ride. Book at least 24 hours ahead.
Late-night arrivals (after 10pm)
Metro Line 2 stops running east from Longyang Road around 10:40pm. Maglev stops at 9:40pm. After that:
Taxi rank — always open, always available (adds 30% night surcharge, so ¥240–320)

Didi — runs 24/7, night pricing adds 20–30%

The night airport bus runs from 23:00 to 05:30, stopping at Longyang Road, Century Avenue and East Yan'an Road on its way to Hongqiao (¥18–34)

If all else fails: there's a 24-hour Sheraton at PVG T2 (¥800–1400) and a Sky Inn capsule hotel airside at T2 (¥280/6 hours)

PVG to Hongqiao (SHA) transfers — the domestic connection trap
Pudong (PVG) is on the east of Shanghai, Hongqiao (SHA) is on the west. They're 60km apart. A domestic flight connection between them can and will eat 3+ hours if you plan it badly.
Transfer options
The Airport Link Line (市域机场线), open since December 27, 2024, is the direct rail link between the two airports. Board at Pudong Airport T1 & T2 station, which connects with the Metro Line 2 and maglev stations; you enter on level 2 and trains leave from the B2 platforms. Trains run every 15 minutes from 06:00 to 22:15 and take about 40 minutes to Hongqiao Airport Terminal 2 station, where you can change to Metro Lines 2, 10 and 17. The full trip costs ¥26. Departures alternate between the platforms on both sides, so check the departure screen before you pick one.

Allow a minimum 4 hours between arrival at PVG and domestic departure at SHA if you're checking bags. If both tickets are on one booking through China Eastern , bags transfer — but you still need to get yourself across town.
Typhoon and delay fallbacks
Shanghai summer (June–September) can shut PVG down for 6–12 hours with typhoons. If your flight is delayed or cancelled:
Check the Feichangzhun (飞常准) or Flightradar24 apps for real-time status

Metro Line 2 keeps running through typhoons unless water floods the tunnel

The maglev stops if crosswinds exceed 25 m/s

Taxi drivers sometimes refuse long runs during red-warning typhoons

Book a nearby PVG hotel (Ramada PVG, Sheraton PVG) via Trip.com for ¥500–900 rather than fight the crowd

Apps and payment
Alipay or WeChat Pay — essential. Use Alipay 's "Tour Pass" feature if you can't set up a Chinese bank card.

Amap (Gaode Ditu, 高德地图) — best map app for Shanghai, English language toggle in settings, integrates with Didi.

Didi — airport pickups, city trips, luxury tier for heavy luggage

Metro Daduhui mini-program (inside Alipay) — tap-to-ride subway

Trip.com — flights, airport transfers, hotels, trains

Shanghai Airport Authority official app — real-time gate info, carousels, delays

Alipay and WeChat Pay accept international Visa/Mastercard in 2026 for most transactions under ¥1000, and unlimited with Alipay Tour Pass. Bring ¥200–500 in cash for the one taxi driver who wants cash and the airport snack kiosk that's offline.

FAQ
Which is the cheapest way from PVG to downtown Shanghai?
Metro Line 2 at ¥8 is by far the cheapest, and it runs directly from PVG to People's Square and Jing'an Temple without a transfer. It takes 75–90 minutes and gets very crowded at rush hour — fine for a solo traveller with a backpack, painful with two suitcases.
Is the Shanghai Maglev worth the ¥50?
For the experience, yes — it's the only commercial high-speed maglev in the world, even though every departure has been capped at 300 km/h since May 1, 2020. Practically, it only saves 20–30 minutes over Metro Line 2 because you still have to transfer at Longyang Road, and a taxi for two costs more per head but takes you to the door. Ride it once going into the city, skip it on the way back.
How much does a taxi from PVG to the Bund cost in 2026?
¥180–240 metered in normal traffic, 45–75 minutes. That includes ¥20 in highway tolls you pay directly. Friday 5–8pm rush or rain can push ¥280 and 90 minutes. Always use the official taxi rank at Arrivals, never the touts inside the terminal.
Can I use Didi at PVG without a Chinese phone number?
Yes, since the Didi International app rollout in 2023. Install "Didi" from the App Store or Google Play before you land, verify with your home phone number, link a Visa/Mastercard or Alipay. Pickup at PVG is from the dedicated Ride-hailing zone in P1/P2 car park, Arrivals level — follow the English signs.
What's the fastest way from PVG to Hongqiao Airport for a domestic connection?
Take the Airport Link Line (市域机场线), the direct train between the two airports: from Pudong Airport T1 & T2 station it leaves every 15 minutes from 06:00 to 22:15, reaches Hongqiao Airport Terminal 2 in about 40 minutes and costs ¥26. Outside those hours, use a taxi, Didi or the night airport bus. Always allow at least 4 hours between arrival at PVG and departure at Hongqiao if you're checking bags and changing airlines.
