Before we go any further: there is no hotel in Beijing called "W Hotel ECLAT." The name shows up in old booking aggregator listings and some AI-generated travel blogs, but it does not exist as an actual property. What exists are two different hotels that first-time visitors often confuse — W Beijing Chang'an , part of the Marriott-owned W brand, and ECLAT Beijing , an independent art-forward luxury boutique. Both sit near Beijing's central business district, both cost about the same on a good night, and both get recommended when first-timers ask for a "design hotel in Beijing." This W Beijing vs ECLAT Beijing guide walks through the real differences in 2026, so you can pick the one that actually fits your trip.
The short version: W Beijing is a classic brand hotel experience — polished, predictable, full-service, with a bigger pool and better fitness center. ECLAT Beijing is an art-stuffed boutique experience — smaller, weirder, more intimate, and packed with original Dali and Warhol pieces in the lobby and corridors. Neither is strictly better. They suit different travelers.

Location: Which Hotel Puts You Where You Want to Be
Both hotels sit inside Beijing's second ring road, in the general CBD area east of the old city. The difference of a few hundred meters matters a lot in Beijing, where traffic can turn a 2-kilometer taxi ride into a 35-minute ordeal.
W Beijing Chang'an
Located on Jianguomennei Avenue, inside the second ring road and closer to the Forbidden City than ECLAT. Walking distance to Jianguomen subway station (Line 1 and Line 2), which makes reaching Tiananmen Square, the Forbidden City, and Wangfujing extremely easy. You can realistically walk to Wangfujing shopping street in about 20 minutes if the weather is nice. For the Great Wall day trips, your driver can reach you without entering the CBD traffic knot.
Downsides: the immediate neighborhood is office-heavy and gets quiet on weekends. Sanlitun nightlife is a 15 to 20-minute taxi ride away.
ECLAT Beijing
Sits inside the Parkview Green complex, a glass-pyramid mixed-use building near the Guomao CBD core. Dongdaqiao (Line 6) is the nearest subway and is a 5-minute walk. From the lobby you walk directly into Parkview Green's retail floors, which include cafes, art galleries, and high-end shops. Sanlitun bar district is 10 minutes by taxi, and the CBD office towers are all around you.
Downsides: you are further from the Forbidden City, and reaching it requires a taxi or a subway transfer. Morning tour pickups from ECLAT have to navigate CBD rush hour.
Verdict : If your Beijing plans lean historical (Forbidden City, Temple of Heaven, hutong walks), W Beijing has a slight edge. If you are here for shopping, nightlife, or business meetings, ECLAT wins.
Rooms: Size, Design, and Tech
W Beijing Chang'an rooms
W Beijing's rooms start at the Wonderful category at 42 square meters, climbing through Spectacular (46 sqm), Marvelous (52 sqm), and a range of suites up to 180 sqm. The design is what you would expect from the W brand in 2026 — playful, color-saturated, with mirrored surfaces, purple and teal accents, and dramatic lighting that can be dimmed through bedside tablet controls. Bathrooms are glass-walled, which looks great in photos and is less great if you are sharing a room with someone you do not know extremely well.
Beds are the W signature pillow-top king and the sound insulation is excellent for a downtown Beijing hotel. Bluetooth speakers in every room. Nespresso machines. A small welcome snack on the desk. The standard Wonderful room is comfortable for two adults but tight if you have more than a carry-on and a backpack.
ECLAT Beijing rooms
ECLAT's rooms are more varied because the hotel is smaller and the building's architecture is unusual. Standard rooms start around 48 square meters — already larger than W Beijing's entry category — and suites climb significantly from there. The design language is the opposite of W's polished brand style: raw concrete accents, hardwood floors, hand-selected art in every room, and some rooms have private terraces overlooking the Parkview Green atrium.
The building itself is wrapped in a glass pyramid structure, and corner rooms get dramatic views into the atrium and out toward the CBD skyline. Bathrooms are generous, with deep soaking tubs in most categories. Beds are firm (more European than American). The rooms feel less like a branded hotel and more like a friend's very expensive Beijing apartment.
Verdict : ECLAT has bigger rooms for the money at the entry level. W has a more consistent luxury-brand feel. Neither is cramped.
Dining and Bars
W Beijing dining
W Beijing runs three main F&B outlets in 2026. YEN on the lobby level does pan-Asian with a focus on Cantonese dim sum at lunch and modern Chinese at dinner, with mains from ¥120 to ¥380. The Kitchen Table on the lobby level is the all-day buffet and breakfast spot — ¥388 for a wide buffet spread including a Japanese station, Chinese congee, and western hot items. The WET deck and lobby bar are the evening social spots, with cocktails from ¥90 to ¥150. Room service is 24 hours.
W Beijing also gets the benefit of Marriott's loyalty program, so gold and platinum status holders receive breakfast, lounge access, and late checkout benefits.
ECLAT Beijing dining
ECLAT is smaller and its F&B is more focused. George's Bar and Restaurant is the marquee outlet and does modern European cuisine with strong wine pairings. Mains in the ¥180 to ¥480 range. Breakfast is served there and is included with most room rates (pay attention when booking — the included rate is worth the extra ¥200 if not).
ECLAT does not have the sprawl of F&B that W offers, which is fine because Parkview Green itself has dozens of restaurants on the retail floors below. You can essentially use the hotel's neighborhood as extended dining.
Verdict : W Beijing has more on-property dining options. ECLAT trades quantity for quality and plugs directly into Parkview Green's food scene.
Pool, Spa, and Fitness
W Beijing has the larger and better-equipped wellness facilities. The pool is a proper indoor lap pool at around 20 meters, with natural light and loungers. The Away Spa has multiple treatment rooms and a full treatment menu. The fitness center is 24-hour access with Technogym equipment.
ECLAT Beijing has a smaller indoor pool (closer to 12 to 15 meters, more for relaxing than laps), a quieter spa with fewer treatment rooms, and a serviceable fitness center. If hotel workouts are important to your trip, W wins clearly.
Service Style and Language
Both hotels handle English-speaking guests without issue. W Beijing staff are trained in the W brand's cheerful, high-energy service style, which some travelers love and others find a bit much after a 14-hour flight. ECLAT Beijing's service is more subdued and Euro-boutique in feel — polite, attentive, less theatrical.
Concierge teams at both properties can arrange Great Wall day trips, private drivers, Forbidden City tickets, and restaurant reservations. ECLAT's concierge is smaller but notably good at arranging art and gallery visits given the building's focus. W Beijing's concierge is better at the standard tourist itinerary and has stronger tour operator relationships.
Price Bands and When to Book
2026 pricing holds roughly to these patterns:
W Beijing Chang'an : ¥1,600 to ¥2,400 weekdays, ¥2,400 to ¥3,500 on weekends and holidays. Suites start around ¥3,800.

ECLAT Beijing : ¥1,200 to ¥1,800 weekdays, ¥1,800 to ¥2,500 weekends. Suites from ¥2,800.

Both hotels run flash sales through Trip.com, Booking.com, and their respective loyalty programs. Book 2 to 3 weeks ahead for the best rack rate. Avoid the first week of May (Labor Day) and the first week of October (National Day) — prices across all Beijing hotels climb significantly during those windows.
Who Should Stay Where: W Beijing vs ECLAT Beijing
Choose W Beijing if:
You are traveling with kids who want a proper pool

You prefer the familiar rhythm of a branded luxury hotel

You have Marriott status and want to use or earn points

The Forbidden City and Tiananmen are high on your list

You want extensive in-house dining options

You are on business and need reliable meeting spaces and business center support

Choose ECLAT Beijing if:
You love contemporary art and want to see Dali, Warhol, and Dia pieces in the lobby on the way to breakfast

You prefer independent boutiques over brand chains

You are a couple on a design-focused trip

You want bigger rooms at a lower starting price

You plan to spend most of your time in CBD, Sanlitun, or Parkview Green itself

You have been to W hotels elsewhere and want something different

Combining the Two: A Split Stay
Some travelers on longer Beijing trips split their stay between the two hotels to get both experiences. A three-night booking at ECLAT followed by three nights at W is surprisingly workable — the taxi between them is 15 to 20 minutes and costs ¥30. You get the design-immersive first half and the full-service second half. Not for everyone, but worth considering if you cannot decide.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is there really no W Hotel ECLAT in Beijing? Correct. The name is a persistent mistake that shows up in AI content and stale listings. The two hotels are completely separate properties run by different companies. Make sure the booking site you are using lists them as distinct options.
How far is W Beijing from Beijing Capital International Airport? About 25 to 30 kilometers, 40 to 60 minutes by taxi depending on traffic. Expressway toll and taxi fare roughly ¥120.
How far is ECLAT Beijing from Beijing Capital International Airport? About 26 kilometers, 40 to 55 minutes by taxi, roughly ¥120 in taxi fare. Similar distance to W.
How far from Beijing Daxing International Airport? Both hotels are roughly 50 to 60 kilometers from Daxing. The Daxing Airport Express plus a subway transfer or a direct taxi (¥200 to ¥260) are your options.
Do either of these hotels accept international credit cards? Yes. Both accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express without issues. They also accept WeChat Pay and Alipay if your account is set up.
Which has a better view? Depends on your room category. High-floor corner suites at W Beijing look toward Chang'an Avenue and the CCTV Tower area. High-floor rooms at ECLAT look into the glass pyramid atrium and out toward the CBD skyline, which is more unusual.
Is the W Beijing pool open late? Yes, typically 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Confirm at check-in as hours occasionally shift for events.
Can I visit ECLAT Beijing's art collection without staying there? Part of the collection is in public spaces inside Parkview Green (below the hotel), which anyone can walk through. The hotel-floor corridors and rooms are restricted to guests.
Late check-in is not an issue at either? Both are 24-hour check-in properties. Notify in advance if you are arriving after midnight to keep your reservation active.
