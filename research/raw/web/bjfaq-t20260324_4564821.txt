I was wondering what the combined ticket include for the Temple of Heaven. Also, can students get the discount tickets?

2026-03-24

Hello!
The Temple of Heaven Park offers free admission to individuals under the age of 18 (excluding 18), who hold valid documents. If you are over 18, you may purchase a discounted ticket at the park's comprehensive service window by presenting your passport and a valid student ID card/certificate (for undergraduate level or below). Alternatively, you can purchase a discounted ticket online through the park's official booking platform (畅游公园) or the official WeChat account of the Temple of Heaven (天坛).
The combo ticket covers general admission and access to three major attractions within the park: the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests, the Echo Wall, and the Circular Mound Altar. If you meet the criteria for discounted tickets, you may purchase a discounted combo ticket costing 17 yuan.
For more information, please call the park's 24-hour service hotline at +86-10-67028866 / 010-67028866 (English services are available from 8:00 to 17:00).
Thank you for your understanding and support!
Beijing Municipal Administration Center of Parks
