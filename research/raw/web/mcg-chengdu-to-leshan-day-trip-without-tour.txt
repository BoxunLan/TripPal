Chengdu to Leshan Day Trip Without Joining a Tour

The Leshan Giant Buddha is the tallest stone Buddha in the world — 71 meters of 8th-century carving cut directly into the cliff face at the confluence of three rivers in southern Sichuan. It is one of the most impressive sights in China, and almost every foreign visitor who sees it does so on a group tour from Chengdu that costs ¥400 to ¥700 per person and runs on a rigid schedule. You do not need any of that. The Chengdu to Leshan day trip is one of the easiest independent day trips in China, thanks to a frequent high-speed rail line, clear signage at Leshan, and a scenic area that is straightforward to navigate without a guide.
This guide walks through exactly how to do the trip on your own in 2026, including how to book the HSR ticket, what to do when you arrive in Leshan, whether to take the boat cruise or walk down the staircase to the Buddha's feet, where to eat lunch, and how to time your return train to be back in Chengdu by dinner. No surprise fees, no tour bus stops at jade factories.

Why a Chengdu to Leshan Day Trip Is Completely Doable Solo
Five reasons independent travelers handle this trip easily:
HSR runs constantly — Trains depart Chengdu East Station for Leshan every 20 to 40 minutes from early morning to late evening. You do not need to book weeks in advance.

Journey time is short — About 1 hour door-to-door on the train, meaning you spend more time at the Buddha than on transport.

Leshan Scenic Area signage is bilingual — English directional signs throughout the main tourist path.

Tickets can be bought at the gate — You do not need to reserve the Buddha entrance in advance except during the peak October and May holiday weeks.

Didi works everywhere in Leshan — If you do not want to figure out local buses, a short Didi ride handles the final leg.

Booking the HSR From Chengdu to Leshan
The fastest trains from Chengdu to Leshan run on the Chengdu-Kunming HSR line and stop at Leshan Station. Most trains depart from Chengdu East Railway Station (成都东站), which is on Metro Line 2 and Line 7. A few earlier trains depart from Chengdu South, but Chengdu East is the one to default to.
Train options in 2026
Duration : Approximately 1 hour (fastest trains are 58 minutes)

Frequency : Roughly every 20 to 40 minutes between 6:30 AM and 9:00 PM

Price : ¥54 to ¥95 for second class, depending on the specific train

First class : ¥85 to ¥150

Business class : ¥170 to ¥295 (not worth it for one hour)

How to book the ticket
You have three realistic options:
Trip.com (easiest for foreigners) : The app lets you book in English with a foreign passport. Service fee around ¥12 to ¥20 per ticket. You collect a QR code that works at the station gate.

China Railway 12306 app or website : The official national system. Supports foreign passports and English now in 2026, though the UX is rougher than Trip.com. No service fee. Best for frequent travelers.

At the station ticket window : Works fine but involves a queue and a basic Chinese conversation. Avoid during peak hours.

Book at least the day before during weekdays. For weekends and holidays book 3 to 5 days ahead to get your preferred departure time.
Arrival: you land at Leshan Station (乐山站)
Leshan Station is NOT in the city center and NOT next to the Buddha. It sits about 12 kilometers north of both. From the station you need either a Didi, taxi, or local bus to reach the Leshan Giant Buddha Scenic Area.
Getting From Leshan HSR Station to the Giant Buddha
Three options in order of ease:
1. Didi or taxi (most convenient)
A Didi from Leshan Station to the North Gate of the Leshan Giant Buddha Scenic Area costs ¥25 to ¥40 and takes 20 to 25 minutes. This is how most independent travelers handle it. Didi works in Leshan without any issues — your Chengdu setup continues to work.
2. Local bus K1 or bus 3
Bus K1 runs directly from Leshan Railway Station to the Leshan Giant Buddha scenic area. Fare is ¥1 to ¥2 and the ride takes 35 to 45 minutes. Bus 3 is a similar route with more stops. Both are fine but add 30 to 40 minutes of total travel time each way compared to a Didi.
3. Tourist bus / shuttle
A dedicated tourist shuttle runs from outside Leshan Station to the scenic area for about ¥10. Less frequent than Didi, but a middle-ground option.
Buying Your Leshan Giant Buddha Ticket
Tickets are purchased at the scenic area gate. You can also book online via Trip.com, Klook, or the Leshan Scenic Area WeChat mini-program, which saves you from the queue at the North Gate ticket office.
2026 ticket prices
Peak season (April 1 to November 30) : ¥80 for adults

Low season (December 1 to March 31) : ¥40 for adults

Student ticket : ¥40 with valid student ID

Boat cruise (optional) : ¥70 separately

Internal shuttle bus : ¥10 one way if you want to skip the uphill walk from the gate

Your passport is required for ticket purchase and entry. Tickets are single-day.
Boat Cruise vs Walking the Stairs: Which Is Better?
This is the core decision of your Chengdu to Leshan day trip. Both options give you a view of the Buddha, but they give very different experiences.
Walking the stairs (the "Nine Turns Cliff Path")
You enter the scenic area, follow the path past the temples and the head of the Buddha, then descend the staircase carved into the cliff face. The staircase zigzags down past the Buddha's side, giving you a close-up view of his ear (which is 7 meters long), the folds of his robes, and the sheer scale of the carving. At the bottom you stand at the Buddha's feet and look up at all 71 meters. Then you climb back up.
Time : 60 to 90 minutes for the full path, depending on crowds on the staircase

Physical demand : Moderate to significant. 200+ steps down and back up, narrow single-file path, can be slow during peak season

Best for : Travelers who want the immersive, close-up experience and are physically comfortable on stairs

The staircase gets congested in peak season. During October 1st holiday and May 1st holiday, the queue to descend can back up for 45 to 90 minutes because the path is single-file. Weekday visits during spring and autumn are much better.
The boat cruise
A tour boat departs from the boat dock downstream of the Buddha and sails directly in front of the confluence, giving you a head-on view of the Buddha from the river. The boat holds about 100 people, stops in the water for 10 to 15 minutes of photos, then returns to the dock.
Time : 40 to 60 minutes total including boarding

Physical demand : None

Best for : Anyone who cannot do stairs, anyone short on time, anyone visiting during peak season when the staircase queue is long

Price : ¥70 per person, paid separately from the scenic area ticket

The view from the boat is the most famous one — it is the angle featured in almost every photo of the Leshan Giant Buddha. You get to see all three carved figures (the Buddha and the two guardian warriors on either side) in a single frame.
Honest recommendation
If you have energy and are visiting off-peak, walk the stairs. The close-up view is genuinely moving and you understand the scale in a way the boat cannot convey. If you are visiting during peak holidays, take the boat first and then walk up to the Buddha's head platform (which does not require descending the stairs). Doing both — boat first, then stairs — is the most complete experience but takes about 3.5 to 4 hours total inside the scenic area.
Lunch in Leshan
Leshan is famous in Sichuan for two things: the Giant Buddha and its food. The city is a major stop on the Sichuan food tourism circuit thanks to dishes like bobo ji (cold chicken skewers in chili oil), douhua fan (silken tofu rice), and qiao jiao niu rou (beef in spicy broth). Skip the food court inside the scenic area — it is overpriced tourist stuff.
Instead, after finishing the scenic area visit, take a Didi or local bus (¥15 to ¥25 by Didi) into Leshan old town. Around Zhangjiadong Pedestrian Street and the Gaobinlu food area you will find dozens of local spots serving the full Leshan food lineup at normal prices.
Suggested lunch budget:
Noodle or rice shop: ¥20 to ¥40 per person

Mid-range Sichuan restaurant: ¥60 to ¥100 per person

Hot pot: ¥80 to ¥150 per person

Sample order at a generic Leshan noodle shop: one bowl of niurou mian (beef noodles), one order of bobo ji, one cold bean noodle dish. You will be full for under ¥60.
Return Train Timing
The last fast train from Leshan back to Chengdu East runs around 9:30 PM, so the return window is generous. For a relaxed Chengdu to Leshan day trip, book a return train between 4:30 PM and 6:30 PM. This gives you enough buffer time for lunch in Leshan old town and accounts for the 20-to-30 minute Didi back to Leshan Station.
Realistic schedule for a comfortable day:
7:30 AM: Depart Chengdu East on HSR

8:30 AM: Arrive Leshan Station

9:00 AM: Arrive Leshan Giant Buddha North Gate

9:15 AM to 12:30 PM: Scenic area visit (Buddha head platform, Nine Turns Cliff Path down)

12:45 PM to 2:00 PM: Lunch in Leshan old town

2:00 PM to 3:00 PM: Optional boat cruise or Wuyou Temple walk

3:30 PM: Didi to Leshan Station

4:30 PM or 5:00 PM: Depart Leshan on HSR

5:30 PM or 6:00 PM: Back at Chengdu East

This leaves plenty of time to return, shower at your hotel, and head out for a Chengdu hot pot dinner.
What to Wear and Bring
Comfortable shoes with grip (the cliff path can be slick)

Light layers — Leshan is warmer and more humid than Chengdu

Small backpack with water

Passport (required for tickets)

Sunscreen and a hat in summer

Portable phone charger

Cash and WeChat Pay or Alipay loaded up

The Leshan Giant Buddha Scenic Area has toilets, snack stands, and water refill points along the main route. You do not need to carry a full lunch.
Combining Leshan With Mount Emei
Mount Emei (Emeishan) is the other major Sichuan landmark within day-trip distance of Chengdu, and it sits only 30 minutes south of Leshan on the same HSR line. Combining both into a single day trip is technically possible but extremely rushed — you will spend 3 hours on trains, 3 hours at Leshan, and maybe 2 hours on Emei, which is not enough to experience either.
The better approach: do the Chengdu to Leshan day trip as its own outing, and do Emei as a separate 2-day overnight trip with a stay in Baoguo Village. Or combine Leshan (half day) and Emei (overnight) on a two-day Sichuan circuit: HSR Chengdu to Leshan morning, Leshan Buddha visit, afternoon HSR Leshan to Emei, overnight on Emei, early hike, HSR Emei to Chengdu.
Frequently Asked Questions
Can I really do a Chengdu to Leshan day trip without any tour? Yes, completely. The HSR system, the scenic area signage, and Didi make it straightforward. Independent travelers from all backgrounds do this trip every day.
Is the Leshan Giant Buddha wheelchair accessible? Partially. The Buddha head platform and the main path near the top are accessible. The cliff path staircase is NOT accessible. The boat cruise is a good option for travelers with mobility limitations.
What is the best season for a Chengdu to Leshan day trip? April to May and September to November. Summer is hot and humid with occasional thunderstorms. Winter is cooler but the low-season entry fee (¥40) makes it tempting.
Are there crowds to avoid? Yes. Labor Day holiday (first week of May) and National Day holiday (first week of October) bring huge crowds to Leshan. The Nine Turns Cliff Path queue can exceed 90 minutes during these weeks. Visit on weekdays or outside holiday periods if possible.
Is there a foreigner discount or free pass? No specific foreigner discounts. Senior travelers (65+) and children may qualify for discounts with ID.
Do I need to book the boat cruise in advance? Usually no. Tickets are sold at the boat dock and depart frequently. On holiday weeks, you may wait for the next boat.
Can I do the Chengdu to Leshan day trip if I don't speak Chinese? Yes. Trip.com is in English, Didi has an English interface, scenic area signs are bilingual, and restaurants in the old town often have picture menus. Offline Google Translate or Pleco on your phone handles the rest.
Is there luggage storage at Leshan Station? Yes. Small lockers are available inside the station for a few yuan per hour. Useful if you are combining the day trip with an onward journey.
What about bathrooms along the Nine Turns Cliff Path? Bathrooms exist at the top of the path and near the Wuyou Temple area but NOT along the cliff descent itself. Use them before starting down.
Can I fly directly into Leshan instead? No. Leshan does not have its own major airport. The nearest airport is Chengdu Shuangliu or Chengdu Tianfu, and HSR is faster from the city to Leshan than flying.
