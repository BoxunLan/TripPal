Hello, there are 2 certified translation offices in Shanghai, are they accredited in Beijing to translate a driver's license to request a temporary driving license in Beijing, and if not, please send an email to an accredited translation office in Beijing, and thank you for the help.

2025-06-20

Hello. According to the Motor Vehicle Administration Office of Beijing Traffic Management Bureau, the "Provisions on Administration of Temporary-Entry Motor Vehicles and Drivers" (临时入境机动车和驾驶人管理规定) and the Regulations on the Application and Use of Motor Vehicle Driver's Licenses (机动车驾驶证申领和使用规定) specify that if your driver's license is not in Chinese, a Chinese translation made by a translation agency or notarized by a notarization agency. There is no restriction on the location of the translation agency, as long as it has the translation service qualification. The translation document of the overseas driving license required by the public to apply for a temporary driving permit in Beijing can be issued by a translation agency certified in Shanghai.
