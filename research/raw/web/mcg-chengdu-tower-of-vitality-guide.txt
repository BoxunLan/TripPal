Chengdu's Tower of Vitality: What It Is and How to See It

The Tower of Vitality (生机之塔) is a group of six lit water columns at the south end of Chengdu SKP, a sunken luxury mall on Tianfu Avenue. Entry is free, the colored lights run from about 19:30 to 22:00, and 30 to 45 minutes is enough. The easiest way there is Metro Line 18 to Jincheng Plaza East, Exit B, a two-minute walk from the columns.
This guide covers the practical side: getting there, timing, photos and where it fits in a day in Chengdu. If you are still deciding whether it deserves a slot in your trip, read our short answer first: Is the Chengdu SKP bamboo tower worth visiting?

What the Tower of Vitality is
The Chinese name is 生机之塔, usually translated as Tower of Vitality and sometimes as Tower of Life. English-speaking visitors often call it the bamboo tower or bamboo fountain, because the dark, segmented columns look like giant bamboo stalks. There is no bamboo in it. The six columns, 26 m and 39 m tall, are aluminum, and design write-ups describe the cladding as recycled aluminum panels.
Unlike a normal fountain, the water runs down the outside of each column from the top. Chengdu press coverage when the mall opened described this as water falling from the sky over the Land of Abundance, an old name for Sichuan. Design press lists 5,328 nozzles and 270 misting nozzles, so the base of each column sits in a cloud of spray. After dark, rings of lights on the columns cycle through four modes: color gradients, flashing, ring bands and single colors.
The columns belong to Chengdu SKP, which opened to the public in December 2022. Most of the mall sits below street level, and the ground above it is a public park. The complex was designed by the architecture studio Sybarite, with landscape design by James Corner Field Operations and lighting by Speirs Major.
Where it is and which metro exit to use
The columns stand at the south end of the SKP complex, beside the SKP-S building, on the east side of Tianfu Avenue in the Jiaozi financial district of the Hi-Tech Zone. The New Century Global Center (环球中心) is across the avenue.
Two metro stations serve it, and they are separate stations: changing between them means leaving one and walking to the other.

Line 18 is the easier choice. From Line 1, Exit E1 is the shortest walk among the station's exits: cross Tianfu Avenue to the SKP side, then follow it south to the plaza. The walking distances come from Amap's route planner.
From the city center
From Tianfu Square, Line 1 runs straight to Jincheng Plaza. For the shorter walk at the end, change to Line 18 at South Railway Station and ride two stops to Jincheng Plaza East. Amap puts either route at 25 to 30 minutes door to door for ¥4. From Nijiaqiao, the station for the Yulin neighborhood, the trip takes about 20 minutes and costs ¥3.
From the airport or Chengdu East Railway Station
Line 18 runs directly from the Tianfu Airport Terminals 1 and 2 station to Jincheng Plaza East, about 45 to 50 minutes for ¥10 by Amap's estimate. From Chengdu East Railway Station, take Line 7 to South Railway Station and change to Line 18; allow about 40 minutes and ¥4.
By taxi or DiDi
Search for 生机之塔 as your destination; Amap lists it as its own place at the south end of the mall. If the app only offers 成都SKP, pick a point on the south side: the complex is large, and a drop-off at its north end adds several hundred meters of walking. Our DiDi guide covers setting up the app with a foreign phone number and card.
Paying for the metro
Since July 2025, every Chengdu Metro station has accepted contactless chip cards issued abroad by Visa, Mastercard and American Express; Diners Club and Discover were added in June 2026. Tap the card on the gate reader going in and again coming out. You can also scan the Chengdu metro QR code in Alipay once a foreign card is linked; our Alipay setup guide covers the linking step.
Last trains on Lines 1 and 18 pass these two stations after 23:00, so there is time to get back to the center after the lights go off.

The mall hours come from the SKP website, which does not publish a schedule for the tower. The light times come from a Chengdu local news account in 2025 and a travel blog in 2026; a few listings give 19:00 as the start. Aim to arrive by 21:00 so that a late start or a crowd does not cut your time short.
How much darkness you get depends on the season. Around the June solstice the sun sets at about 20:10 and twilight lasts until about 20:40, which leaves a little over an hour of real darkness before the 22:00 switch-off. In December twilight ends by about 18:35, so the whole 19:30 to 22:00 window is at night.
During the day you see the columns without the colored lights. That can work for architecture photos, but the lights are the reason most people come.
What to expect on site
The columns rise out of petal-shaped pools, and the misting nozzles spray the area around their bases. Stand close and you will get wet, and the paving gets slippery while the water runs. A small umbrella and shoes with grip help.
Evenings are busy, most of all on weekends and holidays, when photographers and short-video crews set up around the pools. Weekday evenings are calmer. There is no exhibition or ticketed area; the visit is the view itself.
For photos:
A wide lens, or your phone's 0.5x mode, fits a 39 m column from close range.

Plain, dark clothing stands out against the colored lights.

Shoot an hour or two after sunset for a black sky, or at dusk if you want some blue left in it.

Step back across the plaza to fit all six columns in one frame.

The mall has restaurants and cafes, and the BHG Market Place supermarket on level B1 of SKP-S is handy for water and snacks.
How it fits into a day in Chengdu
The tower works best as the last stop of a day spent elsewhere. Three plans that work with the metro:
Pandas in the morning, lights at night
The Panda Base is in the north of the city and is best early: from March to October entry starts at 7:30, and every visitor needs an online real-name booking. Go back to your hotel in the afternoon, then head south in the evening. From Panda Avenue station, Line 3 and then Line 1 reach Jincheng Plaza in about 55 minutes for ¥6. Our guide to Chengdu's panda centers covers tickets and timing.
A neighborhood walk and dinner, then the lights
Spend the late afternoon on the Yulin walk in our Chengdu citywalk guide , eat there, then take Line 1 from Nijiaqiao to Jincheng Plaza, about 20 minutes, to arrive once the lights are on.
The rest of your Chengdu plan
For Jinli, Kuanzhai Alley, Wenshu Monastery and day trips, start with our Chengdu travel guide . The tower fits after dinner on any day you spend in the south of the city, or on a day you pass through South Railway Station, two stops away on Line 18.
