Hello! I would like to purchase tickets for Jingshan Park, but the system shows that my phone number is incorrect. What should foreign visitors do in this case? I would like to book two tickets for the visit.

2025-07-22

Hello. "Changyou Gongyuan" (畅游公园) is the official ticketing platform of the Beijing Municipal Administration Center of Parks. You can purchase the e-tickets of Jingshan Park 1-7 days in advance through its WeChat account or the official website (https://www.bjgyol.com.cn). You can also search for "畅游公园" on your mobile device. It should be noted that you will need to finish real-name verification.

The platform provides services in English and supports ticketing services for holders of passports, Foreign Permanent Resident ID Cards, Residence Permits for Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan Residents, Mainland Travel Permits for Hong Kong and Macao Residents, and Mainland Travel Permits for Taiwan Residents, among others.

If this is your first time buying a ticket to a municipal park in Beijing, you need to use your mobile phone number (which must be registered in the Chinese mainland; no foreign mobile phone number is accepted for the time being) to obtain verification codes for identity binding before purchasing.

For the convenience of visitors, the park offers multiple ticket purchasing options, including the online option and the on-site option at comprehensive service windows. You may choose the option that best suits your needs.

For on-site ticket purchases, you can bring your valid identity document to the comprehensive service window at the entrance of your intended municipal park, and the staff will help you purchase tickets. No real-name appointment or phone number information is required in this case. You can pay with cash (in CNY), BEIJING PASS, and foreign bank cards (such as VISA and Mastercard).

Jingshan Park's 24-hour public service hotline: +86-10-64038098 / 010-64038098 (English services are available from 8:00 to 17:00). You are welcome to contact us at any time.

Thank you for your understanding and support!

Beijing Municipal Administration Center of Parks
